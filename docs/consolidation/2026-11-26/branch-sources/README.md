## Handling rules

- Full QA, hosting, architecture and research JSON documents retain their original relative paths under `pr-NNN/`. Their historical assertions and links are preserved exactly. They are never current runtime, release gates or instructions to execute.
- Modified root canonical master plans, manifests, ledgers, handoffs and READMEs are preserved as exact patches plus complete source references. Their stale whole-file revisions must not overwrite current canonical records.
- Superseded prototype/repair PRs #37, #59 and #76 have exact patches and provenance only. Their runtime is not installed. PR #177 has no changed planning document; its full original description, runtime paths, exact head and source disposition are retained without copying production files.
- The latest owner direction pauses videos and YouTube. Historical references in old research are provenance only. No video asset was retrieved; nothing in this archive enables playback, channels, bookmarks or planned video work.
- The correct way to use the archive is to reconcile unique requirements and evidence into current canonical records, then perform bounded implementation review separately. Never apply all patches or merge all branches together.

- No production runtime, GitHub state, original Workspace, data service, player progress or raw source/video archive was changed or retrieved by this capture.
