## What today's fieldwork establishes

The owner walked Lumiose City and photographed every **permanent gold-chevron Holovator** they could find and unlock. The survey is complete from the owner's perspective, but it does **not** prove that every independently catalogued lift identity has been matched. Keep the existing 21 research entries and avoid turning approximate city-image coordinates into exact device/roof coordinates.

Each documented lift should be explained with the real ground-level entrance, a wide map, a closer neighbourhood/position map, and rooftop arrival photographs where available. The observed lift inside **Wild Zone 6** requires a clear hostile-Pokémon warning. The **Café Cyclone** lift is approached by passing through the large covered arch into the enclosed grassy courtyard; the lift is at the far building wall.

## Ladders and scaffolding: directions, not invented pins

The owner's in-game map screenshots do **not** individually label every ladder, climbable scaffold or rooftop connection. For that reason, do not populate the guide with speculative ladder or scaffold markers.

When a particular climb is independently supported, describe it in an ordinary route card: find the correct street entrance, courtyard or rooftop, inspect the photographed nearby walls and raised platforms, use only the visible accessible ascent, and follow the clearly connected walkable roofs. Distinguish **photographed route**, **inferred route** and **not yet verified**. Do not invent roof crossings, heights or item pickup positions.

A fixed lift is distinct from a ladder or scaffold, and all three differ from moving **DLC Hyperspace distortions**, which must never be treated as permanent map locations.
