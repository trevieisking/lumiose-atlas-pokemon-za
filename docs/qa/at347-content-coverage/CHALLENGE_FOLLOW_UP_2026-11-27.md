# Next batch: Rogue Mega Rush and Defenseless Dodger

This is a follow-up to the pinned PR #224 release `bdfce92bfa7324517d0a1e2f8db7eec84983eefb`, not a change to its Workspace adoption.

- The earlier roster was explicitly unordered. A [firsthand Japanese clear report](https://note.com/riyulun_8win/n/nd0cd72e1d974) agrees with the [numbered reference](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rogue_Mega_Evolution#Rogue_Mega_Rush): Zeraora is fight 22, after Heatran and before Darkrai; Zygarde is fight 27. The clear report also supports bringing one party and using healing moves to conserve the three party heals. Bring a complete Rogue Mega checklist, including EX3. The individual Rogue Mega Simulator's candy payout is separate; no repeat Rush material payout is asserted.
- [GameWith's mission screenshots and route](https://gamewith.jp/pokemon-za/537266) and [Game8's mission screenshots and route](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/749677) agree on Canari and Ivor in Hyperspace Mission 9, 30 seconds, Charge Beam and ₽3,400. Preserve the existing title unlock and repeatability but don't label the first-clear payout as repeatable.
- For SM144, the [Japanese gameplay sequence and reward](https://gamewith.jp/pokemon-za/534892) and [English bench and alternate-prompt walkthrough](https://sgbuzz.com/?p=19015) show two different interactions: inspect the right-hand bench, then use the higher railing's Take Photo prompt. The existing reliable unlock after Hyperspace Mission 3 is unchanged.
- The [official Pokémon overview](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-au/news/adventure-in-style) confirms bench photos for up to six. [In-game control table and Album screenshots](https://altema.jp/legendsza/camera), the [Royale photo workflow](https://gamepedia.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/archives/16629), and the [café partner route](https://gamewith.jp/pokemon-za/525039) support the compact field-camera guide. Café wording follows the in-game prompt rather than assigning a universal button.

## Training and Café Pokémon-Amie
- Two independent illustrated guides, [GameWith](https://gamewith.jp/pokemon-za/522517) and [Gamepedia](https://gamepedia.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/archives/10840), give the end of Main Mission 19 for Hyper Training. [Psypokes](https://psypokes.com/plza/servicenpcs.php) gives the later Mission 37 milestone; the two illustrated accounts outweigh it. - [Plus Moves](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Plus_Move) and [in-game Alpha HP EV evidence](https://gamewith.jp/pokemon-za/526043) support conserving seeds and vitamins. [GameWith's reset walkthrough](https://gamewith.jp/pokemon-za/519109) gives Mission 25; the office is visited in Mission 20, and reports differ on the earliest available reset. Give the early visit and a confirmed later checkpoint rather than guaranteeing the reset on the first visit.
- [Photographed SM025 route and reward](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/side-mission-025-trubblesome-patrons), [independent reward table](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pokémon_Legends:_Z-A/Side_Missions_21-40) and [friendship mechanics](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Friendship): walk the three Trubbish to the marked bins and collect all rewards; cap benefits do not change the evolution threshold.
