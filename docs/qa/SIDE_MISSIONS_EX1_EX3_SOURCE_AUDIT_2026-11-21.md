## Distinct distribution and DLC rules — official sources

1. **EX1, Shine Bright like a Gemstone**: [The Pokémon Company, official 6 November 2025 notice](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/get-diancite-via-mystery-gift-and-diancie-in-pokemon-legends-z-a) says **Diancite from Link Play → Mystery Gift → Get via Internet**; after **completing the base game's main story**, talk to Mimi the Espurr at Looker Bureau, follow her to Diancie, and catch it. **EX1 does NOT require paid Mega Dimension DLC.** The notice does **not** specify Main Mission 39 as a numerical gate, so don't incorrectly present that exact number as directly publisher-confirmed.
2. **EX2, Project M**: [The Pokémon Company, official 2 December 2025 notice](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-gb/news/get-mewtwo-and-mewtonite-stones) says **Mewtwonite X + Y from Link Play → Mystery Gift → Get via Internet**, finish the base story, then enter **Lysandre Café** and reach the **deepest level of Lysandre Labs**. EX2 is playable in the **base game without paid DLC** and leads to Mewtwo. These Mega Stones are gifts *before* the capture, not a drop from defeated Mewtwo. The exact 'begin M39' source wording in B is **not an official stated requirement**; test whether completion of M38 is necessary on a new save.
3. **EX3, Raging Lightning** is a **Mega Dimension DLC** request, unlike EX1/EX2. Corroborated [Serebii EX3](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/raginglightning.shtml), [Bulbapedia mission objective](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)) and [GameTrek EX3](https://gmtreks.com/pokemon-z-a/side-missions/216) give **Hyperspace Mission 12 + EX1 + EX2 + Popping Candy → Plasma-Glazed Donut → Gare de Lumiose screen distortion → Rogue Mega Zeraora**. A valid flavour **threshold** is **Sweet ≥40, Spicy ≥200, Sour ≥400, Bitter ≥280, Fresh ≥40**. The values are minimum *flavour points*, **not exact fixed Berry counts or percentages**. [Bulbapedia Donut chart](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Donut) confirms the same thresholds.
