## High-priority evidence conclusions

1. **Do not publish incorrect battle types:** M22 Mega Barbaracle is **Rock/Fighting** (Grass is 2×), NOT Rock/Water (Grass 4×); M24 Mega Scolipede is **Bug/Poison** (Ground neutral), NOT Ground-weak. These are actual source-content errors, not simply missing detail.
2. **Do not strand players:** M23 mandatory Gwynn battle at blocked Holovator and M26 required Asami and SBC bouts are missing from the examined card narratives.
3. **Protect feedback rows:** Mission 24 post-Corbeau Hotel Z scene and Mission 25 Lida/Naveen combat tips are corroborated as real gaps in B, but may differ in A. The owner's two genuine Supabase reports **#13/#14** remain untouched and open until **both sites** have published and passed these changes.
4. **Preserve accurate details:** source guide's 21 Fire/Ground counter advice, 23 Electric/Dragon counters, 27 Aymlis spelling, 28 Dragon immunity, 29 Grass/Poison counter-types and core 30 tournament bracket are sound. Correct locally without deleting established user guidance.
5. **No false precision:** Textual guide sources support streets and rooftop routes but do **not verify the recovered map's exact coordinate pins or stair heights**. Use approximate pin labels until game screenshot confirms each.
