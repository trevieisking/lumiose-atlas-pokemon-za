**Survey roster:** Helioptile, Drilbur, Onix, Aron, Lairon, Excadrill and Emolga. Compared against the existing fieldbook and matched all seven. Fixed Alpha Excadrill remains a separate entry; the photo does not certify times, weather, floor, repeated-spawn behaviour or Alpha level. [Serebii’s recorded roster](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/wildzones.shtml) corroborates the seven species and Main Mission 20 unlock; [Nintendo Life’s playthrough](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-legends-z-a-wild-zone-14-guide-all-pokemon-and-alphas) corroborates the Magenta district and notes checking for Helioptile on sunny days. Other reports differ on Emolga timing, so no night-only rule is asserted.

**Decision:** Keep the published Magenta classification, and distinguish the nearby Magenta Sector 6 approach from a verified sector polygon or exact gate. No new geometry, pins, photo requests or player progress changes. The full Workspace handoff remains blocked.
