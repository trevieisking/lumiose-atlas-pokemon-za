## Evidence standard

**Official source:** [The Pokémon Company gameplay overview](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay) establishes battles and game mechanics, **not** side-mission teams, streets or specific rewards. For mission-level particulars the audit compares the [Bulbapedia objective/reward table](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)), the [Bulbapedia #081–100 walkthrough](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_Legends:_Z-A/Side_Missions_81-100), [Serebii's individual mission pages](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml), [Thonky's numbered walkthroughs](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/list-of-side-missions), and [PowerPyx reward list](https://www.powerpyx.com/pokemon-legends-z-a-all-side-mission-locations-main-missions/). These are community/independent evidence, **not official publisher instructions**. For conflicts, do not arbitrarily select a reward amount, street or unlock time.

**Common presentation gap:** All twenty reconstructed cards list a subset of reward item names but not the **complete monetary payment and each item quantity** in their displayed reward field. Preserve correct missions, opponent species, player choices and original checkbox IDs; add amounts only with documented evidence and keep disputed numbers flagged.
