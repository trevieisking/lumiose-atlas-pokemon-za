# Alpha Fieldbook: rainy canal and rooftop routes

## Translated route
- [Game8 Goomy](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/731539) and [Game8 Sliggoo](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/731544) place both chance variants along Saison Canal near Jaune 2. Start at Justice Dojo and descend the canal stairs. The reported conditions for this spot are nighttime and rain, with observed Alpha levels 49 (Goomy) and 53 (Sliggoo). [Nintendo Life's independent field guide](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-legends-z-a-where-to-catch-goomy) confirms rainy Goomy in the canal area; it is not independent proof of the exact level, Alpha status or specific night-only slot. Do not generalize this to every Goomy or Sliggoo encounter.
- Game8 identifies a separate daytime/rain Lv. 46 Goomy Alpha at a roof fountain near the Holovator west of Wild Zone 3. [Serebii's mission record](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/abreaktimebattle.shtml) independently confirms Sommi’s Rouge Plaza lift is repaired after Side Mission 4, available at the start of Main Mission 6.
- The same guide identifies Goodra in the post-story Zone 20 Alpha rotation without a rain condition. This is an existing guide destination, not a new city route. Keep the original grouped Zone 20 rendering rather than implying all levels apply to every individual slot.

The export does not encode time/weather in this file; those criteria come from authored field observations. No new exact coordinates, pins, model, map, progress or database changes. This is a presentation-only follow-up on the existing Alpha Fieldbook, not completion of the three Pokédex audits.
