## Evidence tiers

- **P0 / official:** The Pokémon Company [gameplay](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay), [Rogue Mega/Team MZ news](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/news/team-mz), [Mega Evolution](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/mega-pokemon). These support *general mechanics and characters* (real-time combat, Mega Evolution, Challenger Tickets, Rogue Mega rewards). **These pages do not provide every mission step, team or item quantity.**
- **P1 / specialist corroboration:** [Serebii main-mission index](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions.shtml) and individual mission pages, [Thonky detailed walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/), and detailed [Bulbapedia game walkthrough](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_Legends:_Z-A/Part_11). Where two independent guides agree, mark **corroborated secondary**; do not mislabel this as a publisher statement.
- **P2 / in-game check needed:** precise ladder/roof/altitude paths, tiny map-pin positions, exact levels when guides differ, optional rewards and uncertain screen wording. Record these as **unverified** until actual in-game evidence is available.

**Interpretation:** `Verified gap` means the **reviewed branch's specific mission card** is missing a documented player-useful step or explicitly misstates one; **it does not prove the original live ChatGPT Site lacks that material** or that a standalone article did not once contain it. `Needs in-game check` is not authority to edit.

## Verified preservation / no-change findings

- The **official names and mission numbers** for 11–20 match the [Serebii index](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions.shtml); Thonky puts an exclamation mark in its title for 15, but this is not grounds to change the guide's mission title.
- The story's **first-trio Zygarde/pebble encounter already occurs in the recovered Mission 10 card**. Do not add a second contradictory copy as a new Mission 13 event. A short transitional cross-reference may help readers; avoid inventing a different trigger.
- **Mission 19's four hidden-wall investigation objects are already present**, as is Josée's Seed of Mastery and explanation of mastering moves. These should **not** be removed because of older feedback referring to a past version.
- Fire/Ground Camerupt's 4× Water damage, Grass/Poison Victreebel's Fire/Flying/Psychic/Ice weaknesses, the Flying Press hazard and Ghost-type Banette's Dark/Ghost weaknesses are **not demonstrably wrong** from the sources checked. Do not create artificial "fixes" for them.
- Exact entrance **coordinates/roof heights** and random-loot item pins are **not** validated by these textual source comparisons.
