# Wild Zone TM access: all 20 ordinary pickups

26 November 2026. Expands the four-route candidate in PR #188 with the research from PR #189 and the remaining nine Wild Zones. All 20 canonical ledger identities retain their original progress keys and null pins. Three DLC reward TMs are excluded. No physical playthrough or source images were verified, and no exact coordinates are asserted. Sources reopened where available; written clues paraphrased. Historical raw heights are not floor evidence.

## WZ1 — TM103 Work Up (original ID `1-10`)

**Evidence limits:** Roof and ladder corroborated; exact pixel unknown.
