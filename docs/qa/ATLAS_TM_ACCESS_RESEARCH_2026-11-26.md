## WZ4 — TM072 Fire Spin (original ID `4-2`)

Fire Spin: In the southern part of the cemetery, go behind the mausoleum and burn the brambles with a Fire-type move. Collect the yellow TM ball.

**Evidence limits:** Written accounts agree on the southern cemetery, mausoleum and brambles. The historical height value is not a floor measurement.

## WZ11 — TM063 Psychic (original ID `11-9`)

Psychic: Search the northern part of the Wild Zone, on the side of the river where Alpha Slowbro is found. Use a Fire-type move to clear the brambles near it, then collect the yellow TM ball.

**Evidence limits:** English and Japanese accounts identify the Alpha Slowbro area and obstructing brambles. No exact bush or native XY has been visually verified.

## WZ17 — TM062 Flamethrower (original ID `17-6`)

Flamethrower: Once Wild Zone 17 opens, look for a ladder up to the building roof in the northern area. On the roof, search the edge beside a chimney for the yellow TM ball. Watch for the rooftop Alpha Mawile; the exact ladder approach is not yet mapped.

**Evidence limits:** Japanese description places the ladder northwest of the Wild Zone; an English description places a ladder on the northeast side without the same reference frame. Do not assert an exact compass bearing. Chimney-side rooftop and Alpha Mawile are independently described. No new ladder or rooftop pin.

## WZ18 — TM065 Stone Edge (original ID `18-7`)

Stone Edge: Exit through the southwest gate and climb the nearby ladder and scaffolding. Re-enter the Wild Zone at roof level, cross the rooftops to the far side, and look for the golden TM on scaffolding below. Use the roof ledge to line up, then roll and Roto-Glide across.

**Evidence limits:** English and Japanese accounts corroborate the outside southwest approach, elevated traversal and jump/glide to scaffolding. Gamer Guides lists source-space XY 5948,5548; this is NOT a native Atlas coordinate. Exact ladder, ledge and destination positions not verified.
