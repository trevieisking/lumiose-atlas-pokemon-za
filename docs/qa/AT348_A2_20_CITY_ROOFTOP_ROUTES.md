## #10 Fletchling

Start in the central grass of Wild Zone 1. Outside the zones, search along Vert Street, inspect lamp posts by Saison Canal and scan the roofs above the streets. A daytime rooftop sighting is not a universal day-only rule. Other published city areas include Vernal, Estival and Autumnal Avenues, North and South Boulevards, Aymlis Park, Centrico Plaza, Bleu Street, Rouge Street, Jaune Street and Saison Canalside. Sector records: Vert 1–9; Bleu 3–8; Magenta 1–4 and 6–9; Rouge 1, 3 and 5–8; Jaune 2, 3, 5–7, 9, 11 and 12.

Remaining: City levels, exact rooftop approaches, time, weather, unlocks and slot probabilities remain unverified individually; the listed areas are not guaranteed spawns.

## #21 Pidgey

In Wild Zone 1, use Rock Smash to clear the obstacle to the ladder, then climb onto the roof. In the city, look on roofs connected by bridges and the canal-side railings along Magenta Street. Published walking bands across sectors: Vert 1 and 3–9 Lv. 3–16; Bleu 3–8 Lv. 6–9; Magenta 1 and 3 Lv. 7–10; Rouge 1, 3 and 5 Lv. 5–8; Jaune 1–4, 6, 7 and 9 Lv. 6–9. Other listed areas: Vernal and Hibernal Avenues, both boulevards, Aymlis Park, Magenta Plaza, Rouge Plaza, Rouge Street and Jaune Street. The northwest roof in Bleu Sector 2 is a separate reported lead, not corroborated by the other two tables. Wild Zone 1's fixed Alpha is Lv. 24–26, separate from ordinary Pidgey.

Remaining: These are aggregate levels, not slot-specific bands. Individual access, weather, time and spawn odds are unresolved. Bleu Sector 2 remains a lead, not a verified marker.

## #55 Cleffa

You do not need to rely solely on Wild Zone 19. Search the roofs of Rouge Sector 5 (Lv. 11–13) or Magenta Sector 1 (Lv. 12–15). Other city level records: Vert 3 Lv. 10–13, Bleu 4 Lv. 11–14 and Jaune 11 Lv. 11–14. Later, visit the central pavilion in Wild Zone 19 at night. If Cleffa is absent, travel away and return or advance to another night. Approach quietly; a Dusk Ball at night or a Quick Ball on a stealth approach is a possible capture aid.

Remaining: Exact roof approaches, city time and weather, earliest unlock and slot odds remain unverified. The nighttime requirement applies to the documented Wild Zone 19 route, not necessarily every city roof.

## #62 Abra

In Wild Zone 5, head to the northwest corner and climb the ladder to the roofs. Approach quietly; if Abra teleports, search the adjacent rooftops. In the city, search the highest roof in Magenta Sector 6 (Lv. 19–22) or the small roof reached by a ladder in Rouge Sector 6 (Lv. 18–20). Other published city encounters: Vert 8 Lv. 5–7, Magenta 7 Lv. 19–21, Rouge 8 Lv. 18–20 and Jaune 5 Lv. 19–21. A separate written report names the Passage du Palais roof; exact access and its sector remain unresolved.

Remaining: Individual time, weather, earliest access, precise slots and Alpha/respawn odds remain unverified. The combined Lv. 12–26 Wild Zone 5 source band is not ordinary-only; retain the existing detailed zone record.
