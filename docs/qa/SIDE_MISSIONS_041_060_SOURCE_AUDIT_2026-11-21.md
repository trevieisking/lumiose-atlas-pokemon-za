## Method and code-integrity checkpoint

- Official publisher pages [real-time gameplay](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay) and [Mega Evolution](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/mega-pokemon) support broad game mechanics only. Quest details, trigger stages, items, and opponents below come from community/independent documentation: [Bulbapedia mission table](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)), [Thonky numbered walkthroughs](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/list-of-side-missions), and [Serebii side mission directory](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml).
- **Structural source verification:** 203 unique `side-quest` articles total, no duplicate quest numbers, each #041–060 has exactly one article, one expected `data-side-check` and a `side-missions.html#quest-N` link. All twenty reward field labels omit their full monetary payout, even when the core objectives are correct. **This is not verification that the standalone linked article contains full details**.
- **Never equate a community source with an official in-game transcript.** Conflicting unlock stages, minor rewards and pinpoint map geometry stay in the manual verification queue.

## Important source disagreements — DO NOT guess

1. **#043 Rindo Berry**: Bulbapedia and Thonky ×5 vs Serebii ×1, while all sources agree Weedle can be kept/quest rewarded. Treat the **choice** as fixed priority; the exact Berry quantity needs in-game screenshot.
2. **#044–047 timing:** B and Thonky/Serebii use ‘begin M19’, Bulbapedia ‘complete M15’. These may be equivalent around finishing cases 16–18; do not mark B as wrong without gameplay quest-map evidence.
3. **#051 hair-colour availability:** A chain of #012 and Canari rematch in M19 is in B/Bulbapedia, while Thonky claims M27–29 stage. Verify earliest visible mission marker. Nine new colours and Rare Candy ×2 are independently documented.
4. **#052 Candy M**: Serebii and Thonky say **×2**, Bulbapedia says **×3**. Add only after a game screenshot; don't hard-code a guess.
5. **#054/#055 starting point:** Canari vs Gwynn progress inside M19 differs by source. Quest #054 and #055 appear in B after Canari and in Serebii similarly; do not label as confirmed error.
6. **#057 Bleu Sector 1 vs Bleu Sector 3**: two documented location descriptions; use Wild Zone 5 and sewer access route instead of falsely precise map pin.
7. **#041 Vert Sector 9 vs Vert Sector 8**: investigate actual battlefield; correct once supported by in-game map.
8. **#048 ≥100 power** **is verified in Serebii** and should not be deleted as an apparent guess.
