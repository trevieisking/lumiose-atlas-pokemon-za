## Zone 19 — Jaune Sector 8 park

After Jacinthe and Rank B (MM30/31 transition), enter the park near Café Acton. Visit daylight for uncommon Drampa near the pavilion; earlier Atlas evidence specifies morning, while sources give daylight without an exact cutoff. At night search the pavilion for Cleffa and Clefairy. Eastern hedges for Eevee; southern garden near pavilion for Audino; paths/garden for Kangaskhan and ordinary Furfrou. Fixed Alpha Clefairy Lv58–60 at night, Furfrou Lv58–61 during daylight excluding morning per existing Atlas (precise cutoff unverified). GO Hub derivative calls it Clefable in one row; not adopted. Pokémon-France Drampa Alpha inverted range not adopted.

## Zone 20 — Centrico Plaza

Complete MM38 and enter post-story at MM39 transition. The six starters are Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Chikorita, Tepig and Totodile. Nine other ordinary species use grass/path, water or rubble search areas. All fifteen are reported day and night. Chance Alpha rolls after survey completion are distinct from the two rotating slots near Prism Tower ruins; fast travel away and return or advance time to refresh. Retain all 67 rotating candidates, not a guaranteed per-species encounter. Published rotating levels span 63–65; form-specific candidates include Meadow Vivillon, red Florges and medium Gourgeist. Individual rotating level/form projection still open. Zygarde is a scripted MM42 encounter, not a normal or Alpha spawn. No new species-specific pins.
