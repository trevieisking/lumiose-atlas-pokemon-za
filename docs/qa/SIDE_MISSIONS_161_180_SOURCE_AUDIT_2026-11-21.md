## Verification method and source classification

- **Publisher baseline:** [Official Pokémon Mega Dimension information](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-gb/news/new_allies) confirms donuts, Hyperspace and ally roles, *not* quest-specific rewards/locations.
- **Independent game-specific evidence:** [Bulbapedia objective/reward transcription](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)), [Serebii numbered side missions](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml), [Serebii in-game trades](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/ingametrades.shtml), [Dexerto Mega Dimension mission table](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/pokemon-legends-z-a/all-hyperspace-side-missions-walkthrough-in-mega-dimension-dlc/), plus individual links below. **These are not official publisher facts**, and some contain contradictory location metadata; never silently replace B on one conflicting source.
- **Source integrity:** B has 203 distinct quest articles and stable check IDs. Each #161–180 card is a distinct source article with one `data-side-check` and a matching standalone route link. Current compact `Reward:` labels give item names but exclude full quantity/currency. This does not verify 19 missing standalone sources, map coordinates, or Android interaction.
- **No mutations:** Do not merge draft PRs, alter player progress IDs, modify either site, connect a new feedback origin, delete reports, change DNS/IONOS or modify Stream Bandit TCG as part of this audit.
