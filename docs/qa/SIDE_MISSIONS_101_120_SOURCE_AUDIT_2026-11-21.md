## Authority and method

1. **Official publisher/Nintendo** supports overarching mechanics, not every side mission step. [The Pokémon Company: gameplay](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay) and [Ansha/Hoopa official announcement](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-gb/news/new_allies) establish donut-driven Hyperspace and its requirements. [Nintendo UK DLC support](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Purchases-Subscriptions/Games/Unable-to-Access-DLC-or-Upgrade-Pack-Pokemon-Legends-Z-A--2940166.html) explains purchased/installed DLC troubleshooting and distinguishes main-story access from early apparel access.
2. **Specialist independent detail:** [Serebii side missions](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml), [Thonky's 001–119 indexed guides](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/list-of-side-missions), and [Bulbapedia in-game objectives / 101–119 walkthrough](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_Legends:_Z-A/Side_Missions_101-119), with individual references per row. These are not publisher sources. Where they disagree, **flag the conflict rather than infer a correction**.
3. **Structural validation on exact source:** 203 distinct quest article IDs, no duplicates; entries 101–120 each have one `data-side-check=N` and one `side-missions.html#quest-N` crosslink; **all 20 reward labels show item names but not cash/complete quantities**. This structural test does **not** prove the placeholder standalone article routes are complete or that any source-claim is accurate.
4. **Status vocabulary:** *Confirmed current-B omission* = content visibly absent from inspected source and supported externally. *Source conflict* = credible independent evidence disagrees; do not treat either variant as game-verified. *Preserve* = current B fact corroborated, do not rewrite gratuitously. Coordinates and game dialogue need manual gameplay validation.
