## Independent checks and findings

Actual Bulbapedia HTML was retrieved independently, and its labelled Day/Night/Clear/Harsh sunlight/Cloudy/Rain/Fog columns were expanded using the table cell spans. Ordinary rows were kept separate from the fixed-Alpha heading. All 30 time-condition IDs in the amendment agree with those ordinary-table cells. Helioptile's daytime condition was already present in the reviewed earlier runtime, so the packet correctly distinguishes 30 comparisons against the original archive from 29 new time conditions against that later checkpoint.

Fresh [RotomLabs Wild Zone 3](https://rotomlabs.net/locations/legends-z-a/wild-zone-3), [Wild Zone 7](https://rotomlabs.net/locations/legends-z-a/wild-zone-7) and [Wild Zone 16](https://rotomlabs.net/locations/legends-z-a/wild-zone-16) Walking tables agree with exactly the five promoted forms: red Flabébé, red/orange Floette and blue/white Florges. Blue/white Flabébé remain leads. The pages' separate Alpha rows were not treated as ordinary spawns or proof of fixed-Alpha status.

The explicit Japanese ordinary-entry conditions corroborate the amended [Wild Zone 15](https://pente.koro-pokemon.com/legendsz-a/map-wild-zone-15.shtml), [Wild Zone 17](https://pente.koro-pokemon.com/legendsz-a/map-wild-zone-17.shtml) and [Wild Zone 18](https://pente.koro-pokemon.com/legendsz-a/map-wild-zone-18.shtml) times and rooftop notes. [PowerPyx Scyther](https://www.powerpyx.com/pokemon-legends-z-a-scyther-location/) independently reports Wild Zone 13 at night, and [PowerPyx Florges](https://www.powerpyx.com/pokemon-legends-z-a-florges-location/) reports Wild Zone 16 in daytime. [Serebii's weather page](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/weather.shtml) explicitly excludes Skarmory during rain. The runtime's narrower no-rain wording appropriately preserves uncertainty about other weather restrictions. Rooftop notes remain attributed and do not invent exact floor numbers, coordinates or a universal route prerequisite.

[Thonky's Side Mission 008 walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/side-mission-008-get-well-fennekin) supports Branche northwest of Prism Tower, the Café Classe enquiry, the Wild Zone 5 waterway/tunnel/ladder route and the monetary/candy rewards. [Altissimo's Magenta Sector 2 page](https://altissimo1.github.io/Supplementary-Series/Legends/Lumiose-Locations/magenta-sector-2.html) independently supplies Branche, Main Mission 6 availability and the level 14 Fennekin gift. The corrected mission keeps the gift separate from repeatable ordinary encounters.

The ordinary Ampharos claim remains disputed and is separate from the fixed Alpha. Other previously disputed standard-roster claims, the source-reported Gourgeist-size disagreement and the Wild Zone 20 rotating pool remain qualified. Omission from another source was not used as proof of absence. The combined roster review must be judged against supported searchable records, established conditions/class and clearly labelled disagreements; an exhaustive absence proof is not required by this receipt.

An adjacent, pre-existing Quest 011 sentence asserted evolution during the battle. Thonky and the [Bulbapedia trainer roster](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)/Trainers) agree on two level 8 Kakuna, one level 9 Kakuna and one level 11 Beedrill. [Serebii's mission prose](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/thekakunamaster.shtml) is imprecise about evolution timing, while [Phrasemaker's written walkthrough](https://thephrasemaker.com/2025/10/19/pokemon-legends-z-a-how-to-complete-the-kakuna-master-side-mission-011/) places a further evolution after the win. The release owner was asked to use the confirmed team and omit timing. This adjacent correction is separate from approval of the encounter amendment; this documentation-only commit makes no runtime change.
