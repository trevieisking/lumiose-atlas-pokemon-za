## Evidence distinctions that remain visible

The Passage Ombragé sources identify different ladder sides. This could describe multiple entrances; it is not evidence that either individual account is false. The new route retains both descriptions and chooses no exact entrance. The source map crop does not show enough detail to settle that question.

The mission-80 route is one documented way to approach the Jaune 7 courtyard. Neither the packet nor the route says that mission 80 is the only possible way onto those roofs. The original Hibernal Avenue owner gallery is linked for orientation and is **not** identified as the mission device. Existing mission-register uncertainty remains unchanged.

The Froakie route separates its Jaune starting courses from the final course farther north and retains the published Roto-Glide requirement for that final stage. Jaune 10's scripted story chase is not promoted into an independently traversable free-roam rooftop route.

All sixteen distinct original sector map crops used by this batch were visually inspected, including the shared Jaune 9–12 crop. Seven existing owner galleries supplied fourteen inspected ground/arrival images. A view of a local arrival supports recognition; it does not prove an unphotographed roof crossing. No original image bytes, photo metadata or gallery identifiers changed.

External screw numbers have not been copied into the original 100 collectible IDs. All new lead coordinates, canonical screw IDs and exact floors remain null, and `publish_new_pin` remains false. No videos were used or added.
