## Method and evidence limits

The actual HTML of all 20 current [Bulbapedia Wild Zone tables](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wild_Zone_1) and all 20 [Serebii zone tables](https://www.serebii.net/pokearth/lumiosecity/wildzone1.shtml) was fetched and compared. Their per-page URLs and SHA256 receipts appear in [source-review.json](source-review.json). Bulbapedia's image headers were read as Day, Night, Clear, Harsh sunlight, Cloudy, Rain and Fog; each row's `colspan` was expanded before interpreting a check mark. An unlabelled flattened search-result check mark was not treated as a time or weather claim.

Species names were compared within their encounter sections. Alpha icons and the separately labelled chance rows were inspected in Serebii's HTML. Wild Zone 20's generic Alpha heading was not used to convert its rotating pool into fixed positions. The owner's twenty original survey images and their 140 species observations retain the exact existing paths, hashes and AT339 provenance. A missing icon or missing table row does not prove absence, and two pages may share upstream data.

The 48 sector records received an evidence-coverage and classification review of the preserved register. This pass does **not** claim that every linked sector source was freshly reopened or that every roof, interior, side mission and ordinary encounter was re-observed. Their precise outstanding row IDs are retained in the per-area review. The two empty sectors, Bleu 10 and Magenta 5, remain unknown; nearby Wild Zone lists are not copied into them.

## Required guide correction: Side Mission 008

The original source section, generated home guide, and standalone side-mission guide named **Jardin** as the Fennekin giver and omitted the usable access route and reward quantities. [Thonky's mission walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/side-mission-008-get-well-fennekin) and [Altissimo's firsthand mission account](https://altissimo1.github.io/Supplementary-Series/Legends/Legends-Z-A/side-missions.html) identify **Branche**. The corrected guide includes the water-cleared waterway/tunnel, north ladder and twig distinction, return step, level-14 gift, ₽1,200 and candy quantities. It preserves quest ID 8, the progress key, checkbox behaviour and local navigation.

[Serebii's mission page](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/getwell,fennekin.shtml) repeats the incorrect giver name. StrategyWiki's search result gives a differing cash amount, while the two authored walkthroughs agree on ₽1,200. These conflicts were checked rather than copied into the site. The confirmed gift remains a `gift`; it cannot authorize ordinary Magenta Sector 2 habitat.
