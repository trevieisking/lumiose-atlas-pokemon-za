Serebii's spawn volume distinction implies separate fields for area, spawn centre, possible radius/extent, vertical floor, condition, and encounter class. Never convert a mission enemy, gift or rotating Alpha into a permanent standard spawn. Separate one-way traversal, ladders, Holovators, glide and prerequisite access from simple highlighted areas.

## Wider game research register

- **Progression:** Thonky main-mission list and Serebii mission indices support 42 main missions, 119 numbered base side missions and 2 base EX missions, 120–200 DLC numbered side missions plus EX3; 14 Hyperspace missions. These are source-derived targets for an index-by-index audit, not a claim that every objective has been checked. DLC story requires main story completion. Distinguish post-story tasks from final DLC credits. Existing walkthroughs stay unchanged.
- **Pokédex:** English/French sources indicate 232 base entries and 132 Hyperspace entries; older pre-DLC guides report different counts. Verify distinct entry IDs/forms and overlap before changing totals. Do not simply add counts as a unique species count.
- **Alphas and shinies:** Separate fixed Alpha from random eligible spawns after zone survey/progression; sources describe mastered moves and special IV/EV properties. Exact random odds, charm and donut modifiers need independent verification; preserve existing guaranteed rows. Distinguish saved city shiny encounters from time-limited Hyperspace catches.
- **Mable and collectables:** Research tasks, research points and rewards (including shiny charm); 100 Colorful Screws and plush rewards at Racine. Verify tier costs and reward descriptions separately from provider pin numbering. Preserve Atlas's existing 100 screw IDs.
- **Traversal and combat:** Mastered/Plus Moves, Seeds of Mastery with Josée at Justice Dojo, obstacle clearing, weather/time, glide and story-gated roof routes are independent systems. Document exact move prerequisites, entrances, exits, and one-way gaps using owner observations. No inferred connections between adjacent roofs.
- **Mega Dimension:** Official DLC supports Hyperspace Lumiose and level boosts beyond 100. Donut effects, berry ingredients, survey objectives, bonus balls and special scans need dedicated guides and version-aware evidence. Temporary distortions are session state, not permanent map pins. Five-star difficulty/level recommendations are guide estimates until checked. Do not freeze random donut results into deterministic recipes.
- **Patch corrections:** Nintendo lists 2.0.1 changes to bulk berry buying, Truck No. 3 berry sales after DLC starts, and Mega Shard cap 9,999 (formerly 999), plus weather/target direction, Mega Pokédex shiny registration and Side Mission 188 fixes. Version 2.0.2 is a further issue-fix release. See official notes above.
- **HOME:** Research compatibility and gift conditions using current official Pokémon HOME help before publication. Alpha starters can be a separate gift category, not a wild spawn or an assumption of every save. Preserve transfer direction and eligibility restrictions; do not encourage a new account.
