# AT-346: Fossil Alpha routes

Run `python3 scripts/audit_fossil_alpha_routes.py INPUT_DIR` with the [pinned Hyperspace inputs](HYPERSPACE_GENERATED_WILD_AUDIT.md). Eleven source rows give eight unique positive typed routes. The two 3★ Rock species occur in disjoint patterns (Tyrunt 3/6, Amaura 4/7). The 4★ Ice Amaura is an alternative, not a prerequisite. The 5★ Rock pattern covers all five species across three clusters, without guaranteeing each one on a visit. Capture levels are distinct from combat levels, and Alpha adds nine within these typed routes.

The level-39 day/night evolution methods are verified in the pinned personal data. The base-game lab route is separate: after Side Mission 27, Reg takes the matching fossil. Repeat ordinary revivals are Lv. 20, Alpha revivals are Lv. 35 and have a reported 1% rate per revival. The in-game prices are ₽20,000 for Jaw/Sail Fossils and ₽30,000 for Old Amber. Sources: [3★ guide](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/749781), [Rock](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/hyperspacewildzone/rock.shtml), [Ice](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/hyperspacewildzone/ice.shtml), [Special Scans](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/startspecialscanning.shtml), [Lab records](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/giftpokemon.shtml), [Stone Emporium field guide](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/pokemon-legends-za-fossils-amber-tyrunt-amaura-aerodactyl/1100-6535466/). No maps, roster changes, real-world spend or publication.
