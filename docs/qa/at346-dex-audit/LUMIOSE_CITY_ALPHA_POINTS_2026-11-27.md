# AT-346: City Alpha spawn conditions

The pinned [point source](https://github.com/Xzonn/PokemonLZA-pkNX/blob/eadfb4b3ee9b1e09bb217dda87f1acd796e1a31f/spawner/t1_point_spawners_a.json) and [named slot source](https://github.com/Xzonn/PokemonLZA-pkNX/blob/eadfb4b3ee9b1e09bb217dda87f1acd796e1a31f/spawner/t1_slot_sets.json) agree exactly as multisets by non-Wild-Zone location, National species ID, form and caught level range: 114 positive Alpha children from 81 physical points, yielding 103 distinct leads across 43 labels and 25 species/form combinations. To replay, save those files as `point.json` and `slots.json` in one folder and run `python3 scripts/audit_city_alpha_points.py PATH_TO_FOLDER`. The script checks exact Git blob hashes. Alpha-100 on a child does not imply that species is always selected or imply odds per visit. No geometry or technical phase flags were imported into player data.

- **Café Kizuna, Jaune 5**: `spn_outzone_a0501_K00` has the only positive Dratini child for this location. The parent specifies daytime and sunny, clear-sunny or rainbow conditions. Base Lv. 28–29, Alpha Lv. 38–39. The [garden ladder and roof route](https://www.gamerguides.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/guide/getting-started/pokemon-locations/where-to-find-scraggy-in-legends-z-a) is independently described. There is no evidence to apply this time gate to every Dratini roof.
- **Restaurant Le Nah, Vert 6**: `spn_outzone_a0101_K50` is a separate Dratini/Dragonair point. Dragonair is Lv. 30–32 ordinarily, Lv. 40–42 if Alpha. This imports the verified written route from the earlier Dragonair candidate; its access and obedience notes remain scoped to that rooftop.
- **Justice Dojo / Saison Canal**: `spn_outzone_a0502_L01` and `L03` have rain gates and no explicit time filter. Both support Goomy Lv. 45–49 or Sliggoo Lv. 51–53 when Alpha. Game8 observed [Goomy Lv. 49](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/731539) and [Sliggoo Lv. 53](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/731544) in rainy weather at night, with named stair access and a Dojo fast-travel loop. The guide uses night as an observation, not a requirement; no independent daytime sighting is claimed at these two points. A separate rainy daytime Goomy at the rooftop fountain west of WZ3 is documented in the same firsthand guide.
- **Other cohorts**: The four existing rooftop Riolu routes agree with the clear-daylight group. The two published Dedenne locations have no explicit weather/hour restriction in this export, so their current observed labels are not promoted to requirements.

Written Fieldbook only. No new pins, changes to maps/photos, encounter odds or claim that the full city Alpha audit is complete.
