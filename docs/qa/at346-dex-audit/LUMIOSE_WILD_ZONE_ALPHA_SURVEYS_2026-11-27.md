# AT-346: Existing Wild Zone Alphas and survey gates

The [point export](https://github.com/Xzonn/PokemonLZA-pkNX/blob/eadfb4b3ee9b1e09bb217dda87f1acd796e1a31f/spawner/t1_point_spawners_a.json) and [encounter table](https://github.com/Xzonn/PokemonLZA-pkNX/blob/eadfb4b3ee9b1e09bb217dda87f1acd796e1a31f/raw/world/ik_data/field/pokemon/encount_data/encount_data/encount_data_array.json) are pinned. With the source files downloaded locally, run `python3 scripts/audit_wild_zone_alpha_surveys.py POINT_JSON ENCOUNTER_JSON`. The script checks each exact blob, matches the guide roster by zone, National species name and calculated Alpha range, and preserves duplicate encounters as separate rows.

- All 38 published pre–Wild Zone 20 fixed rows match by zone, species and levels. This cohort excludes the three one-time Mission 40 encounters and the two central Wild Zone 20 pools (134 rows).
- All 439 conditional Alpha rows specify completion of their own Wild Zone survey. This verifies the condition for eligible encounters, not the odds per visit or every ordinary spawn. A survey gate can exist on a fixed Alpha as well.
- **Wild Zone 15:** The fixed Lv. 52–53 Gourgeist in the central grass has the WZ15 survey gate in the encounter activation table. It has no day or weather restriction on the point; only the seven ordinary species are survey targets. The encounter is now independently identified as Medium Variety; see the update below. The guide does not assume a separate research reward-claim step. Cross-checks: [field screenshot](https://game8.jp/pokemon-legends-z-a/731207), [day and night](https://www.powerpyx.com/pokemon-legends-z-a-gourgeist-location/), [Mable’s research](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mablesresearch.shtml), [survey roster](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/pokemon-legends-z-a-wild-zone-15/).
- **Wild Zone 17:** Both Lv. 56–57 Alpha Pyroar slots are male. Their day and night points share one encounter ID, but are separate records. Neither is the ordinary female Pyroar. [Zone roster](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wild_Zone_17).
- WZ11 Clawitzer and WZ3 Fletchling have other raw work conditions; their exact player-visible changes remain open. No new directions inferred from those flags.

Guide-only corrections. No map coordinates, photo changes, or new encounter rows in player data.

## Variety verification and Side Mission 98

- **Mission 98:** The [firsthand walkthrough](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-legends-z-a-how-to-find-a-jumbo-pumpkaboo) and [mission listing](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/jumbovarietypumpkaboo.shtml) agree on a non-Alpha Jumbo at night, the handover and item quantities. The first also documents the money reward, Summary check and difference between Jumbo Variety and XL. The existing unlock wording follows the latter listing.
- **Wild Zone 15:** All seven Pumpkaboo point objects contain four positive Alpha rows. Medium (0) and Small (1) are day; Large (2) and Jumbo (3) are night. Ordinary is Lv. 42–43, Alpha Lv. 52–53 after the survey. Passing the optional pinned [personal JSON](https://github.com/Xzonn/PokemonLZA-pkNX/blob/eadfb4b3ee9b1e09bb217dda87f1acd796e1a31f/raw/avalon/data/personal_array.json) as the third script argument verifies all four same-form trade evolution destinations.
- **Fixed Gourgeist:** Raw National 711 form 0 and the personal dimensions (0.9 m, 12.5 kg) match the [exact encounter entry for Medium Variety](https://rotomlabs.net/dex/mega-dimension/gourgeist/average-size), called Average Size in older games. WZ20's central Gourgeist is likewise form 0. This resolves conflicting size labels from earlier described sightings without generalizing to every Gourgeist spawn.
- **Generated typed Hyperspace:** The separate [reproducible typed join](HYPERSPACE_GENERATED_WILD_AUDIT.md) yields only form 0 for National IDs 710 and 711: 1★ Ghost Pumpkaboo Lv. 17–19, 4★ Ghost/Grass Pumpkaboo Lv. 36–38 and Gourgeist Lv. 39–41. Eligible Alpha results are +9. This does not establish the forms used by other Hyperspace encounter classes. The guide recommends the directly verified WZ15 Pumpkaboo plus trade route for other Alpha varieties, without changing the map or generated portal data.
