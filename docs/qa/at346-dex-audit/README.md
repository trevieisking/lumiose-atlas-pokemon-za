## Implementation order and acceptance

1. **Lumiose Pokédex** — 232 identities and all relevant forms. Audit every wild habitat (all 48 sectors, 20 Wild Zones, roof, court, alley, interior, sewer, lab, and special location), all non-wild methods, and all known conditions. Include named landmarks, approach directions, access prerequisites, spawn prerequisites, levels, day/night/weather, form, repeatability, mission/rank, and capture advice. Distinguish gifts, evolution, trades, events, transfer and catchable mission encounters from repeatable wild catches. Preserve original dex cards, types, stats, art, IDs and links.
2. **Hyperspace Pokédex** — 132 identities plus applicable base-dex encounters and forms. Research all portal types, star thresholds, story progression, scans, donut effects and conditional encounters. Separate guaranteed/scripted and random outcomes; give source-qualified ways to find a suitable portal rather than permanent pins. Maintain existing DLC guide and links.
3. **Alpha Pokédex (Atlas-specific catalogue, not a third official numbered regional dex)** — evaluate all 364 National species and relevant forms, including exclusions. Classify verified direct fixed, random, survey-dependent, rotating, mission-only, Hyperspace, fossil, gift, evolution, and transfer routes; mark unavailable and uncertain explicitly. Do not infer every ordinary spawn has an Alpha variant. Give all independently supported locations and conditions rather than one location per Pokémon. Preserve the Alpha Fieldbook's existing controls and evidence; reuse it for the new searchable species/form catalogue.
4. **One canonical encounter contract** — extend the existing `atlas-area-model.mjs`, `atlas-area-ui.mjs`, `atlas-area-surfaces.mjs` and `atlas-area-evidence.json` without discarding stable area/species/progress IDs. The current model lacks a distinct random_alpha class. Design and test that class separately from fixed_alpha and rotating_alpha, along with any non-wild methods; preserve legacy consumers. Use one-to-many rows keyed by stable National number/form and independent encounter IDs, with scene, district/sector/zone/landmark, acquisition class, Alpha mechanism, level, time, weather, floor, progression and access requirements separately, repeatability, original written approach, evidence refs/language, date/version, confidence and explicit unresolved fields. Distinguish unknown from unrestricted. Link existing two dex switches and Alpha Fieldbook to map search and matching source-qualified area highlights. Only plot exact coordinates or geometry after existing calibration and review gates; no inferred point from a roof/sector name, no permanent random portal marker, no fabricated paths.
5. **Worldwide sources and QA** — prioritize game/official records and independently authored Japanese, English, French, German, Spanish, Korean and Chinese evidence when available; translate official names into English, record source-specific disagreements, avoid bulk copying external maps or guide prose. Audit numbered entries and separate forms; log each method and location with provenance. Validate all cross-links, filter states, unknown/non-wild handling, original source images, map gestures and player progress. Require exact-head automated tests, phone/desktop visual review, matching GitHub merge/deployment/live verification and independent original-Site import/backup/rollback before marking either platform verified. No public feedback, player data or Supabase schema changes. No Code God, CG Repair Lab, Repo Desk or Writer.
