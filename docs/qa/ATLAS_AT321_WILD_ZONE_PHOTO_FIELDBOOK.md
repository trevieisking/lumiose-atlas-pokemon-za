# AT-321 Wild Zone photograph and encounter fieldbook

Owner-approved 25 November 2026. The 40 original 1536 × 865 PNGs are preserved byte-for-byte. Named areas are from the four owner asset manifests; district and unlock requirements remain the original Atlas map records. Map photos are session evidence only, not player progress or exact item coordinates.

The catchable species roster for zones 1–19 includes documented ordinary and fixed Alpha encounters; it is deliberately not labelled all ordinary or all simultaneous. Source: Serebii Wild Zones (https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/wildzones.shtml), checked 25 November 2026. Serebii guaranteed Alpha data (https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/alphapokemon.shtml) aligns with the preserved Atlas fieldbook; the 38 original rows and their levels/time notes are copied from `src/sections/alphas.html` without changing their existing values. Zone 20's 15 ordinary species are separately cross-checked against Nintendo Life (https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-legends-z-a-wild-zone-20-guide-all-pokemon-and-alphas) and Pokémon GO Hub (https://pokemongohub.net/post/guide/all-wild-zones-pokemon-legends-z-a/). The 67-species rotating pool is preserved from the Atlas source, including its level range and two rotating encounters; it is not 67 guaranteed spawns. The encounter listing does not certify detailed conditions for each ordinary species; no such conditions are invented. Source references are provenance, not external player navigation.

One canonical JS module powers the main guide and Explorer; static map and directory panels are generated from its identical markup, with parity and hash tests. Original pages, IDs, Alpha/item clues, sector photographs, routes, feedback, player progress and original Workspace are unchanged. The gallery uses no additional photos or external calls. Exact-head CI and phone/desktop screenshot review must pass before promotion.
