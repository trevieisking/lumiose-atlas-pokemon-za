## Evidence methodology and integrity

- **Publisher/official:** The Pokémon Company's [gameplay overview](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay) and [Mega Evolution](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/mega-pokemon) establish mechanics such as real-time Pokémon battles and holding a Mega Stone. **Official pages do not publish detailed instructions, named NPCs, level-by-level rewards, or exact mission triggers for all twenty requests.**
- **Specialist sources:** The [Bulbapedia transcription of mission objectives and payouts](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)), [Serebii's side mission database](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml), [Thonky's numbered walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/list-of-side-missions), and [RPG Site reward list](https://www.rpgsite.net/guide/18713-pokemon-legends-z-a-side-mission-list-rewards). These are independent community/editorial data, **not official Nintendo statements**. For material discrepancies, keep unresolved until direct gameplay checks.
- **Structural validation of exact GitHub source:** `src/sections/sidequests.html` contains **203 unique quest articles**, no duplicate article quest numbers; #021–040 occur once each and each has the expected **one `data-side-check` ID** and matching `side-missions.html#quest-NN` link. This preserves checklist structure, **not link target content completeness**.
- **Common missing rewards:** Every one of #021–040 lists reward *names* but **does not show full quantities and payout money** in the reward field; #027 mentions the separate ₽20,000 fossil purchasing subsidy in *steps*, which is not its quest completion payout. Record money, items, gifts, feature unlocks and cosmetics as distinct reward types to avoid misleading users.

## High-confidence remediation queue (do not edit a live site from this document)

1. **Universal reward schema:** full completion cash + item quantities + gifted Pokémon + abilities or facility access + cosmetics. The existing B labels mask these distinctions in all 20 entries. Keep quest progress IDs unchanged.
2. **Gameplay blockers / valuable directions:** #021 search all museum exhibits; #025 follow Trubbish; #027 distinguish fossil subsidy vs reward; #033 reach Alpha Magikarp beyond water/rocks; #037 cannot catch encounter Binacle; #040 repair the correct Holovator.
3. **Choice / one-time rewards:** #022 gives only **one** Kanto starter; #024 is an actual Abra exchange; #027 choose Jaw OR Sail initially; #039 normal-to-Galarian Slowpoke trade.
4. **Cosmetics crosslinks:** #030–032 should appear in the personalisation/contact-lenses section (if one exists), not be missed because they are absent from ordinary item reward labels.
5. **Hold disputed claims** until game evidence: #021 Riolu/Lucario statue reference; #026 extra Burn Heal; #030/#033/#034 unlock after Main Mission 10 vs start Mission 14; #037 exact number of Binacle; #039 Power Belt quantity; #040 precise street/sector transition. No arbitrary guess is publishable as a verified fact.
6. **Integrity:** every reviewed card has the correct one `data-side-check` and `side-missions.html#quest-NN` link. **Do not rename IDs or bulk regenerate checklists without tests**, especially with the dependent map and progress-import PRs.
