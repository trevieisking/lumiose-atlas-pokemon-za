## Evidence hierarchy and limitations

- The [official Pokémon gameplay guide](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay) confirms high-level real-time combat, Mega Evolution, promotion matches and tickets. For *precise mission steps*, the primary-reference source here is the [Bulbapedia transcription of in-game mission objectives](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)); check [Serebii's mission index/details](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions.shtml) and [Thonky's step walkthroughs](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/) for complementary independent evidence.
- [Nintendo official Z-A DLC support](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/68630/p/1076/c/120) establishes the DLC prompt leading to **Hotel Z rooftop**, after the prerequisite content. Distinguish base-game postgame quests from Mega Dimension DLC.
- Edition A, the original ChatGPT Workspace site, still cannot be source-edited or independently certified here. All findings refer to **Edition B's reviewed mission cards only**, unless otherwise noted.
- A generic ‘follow the marker’ or type tip is not full walkthrough coverage; a card can be accurate but incomplete. Do not delete correct content.

## Cross-batch correction: Roto-Glide classification

**Earlier M15/16 note AT-025 has been revised:** [Bulbapedia's recorded Mission 15 objective list](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)) explicitly includes **meeting Taunie/Urbain on the rooftop and using Roto-Glide** while M15 is active. Other guides classify the tutorial under Beedrill M16, which is available in parallel. Do **not** call its placement in M15 a confirmed factual error. Add ‘speak to Lida to start strategy meeting’ and explain the overlap. [Neoseeker M15–18](https://www.neoseeker.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/Main_Mission_15-18) and [Bulbapedia objectives](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)) support this nuance.

## Verified preservation and release conditions

- Major story sequence for M31–42 in B is largely present, and M37's Zygarde shields, M38's DLC rooftop destination, M40 Alpha zones, M41 Yveltal destination and M42 encounter phases are **not** reasons to erase the existing guide. Most necessary changes are additive.
- **Do not mix milestone 15 Infinite Royale wins with the trigger for the legendary quests**. This is a player-progress blocker. The postgame base-game M38 / optional DLC progression / Royale rewards / legendaries should be distinct UI flows.
- **Do not hide conditional access** for the M34 Holovator (Side Mission 092) or for other rooftop climbs.
- The base-game main story **ends with credits at M37**, and M38–42 are postgame. Mega Dimension DLC access is a separate opt-in following main story completion; Nintendo's official instructions should be followed where possible.
- Source audit is **not** a release certificate: mission card/source-based only, exact coordinates, game-specific dialogue and Android behaviors require manual checking. No changes to original Workspace site A, independent guide gameplay, Supabase feedback rows, DNS, IONOS, Stream Bandit TCG or draft PR branches in this batch.
