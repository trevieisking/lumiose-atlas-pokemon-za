## Routes, unlocks and conditions

For **Restaurant Le Nah**, the guides describe an adjacent-building ladder and crossing the gap. Game8, VGC and [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/pokemon-legends-z-a-dratini-location/) allow a roll or rolling jump. Roto-Glide is an alternative; do not impose Main Mission 16 as a universal restaurant-route prerequisite. GameWith describes this restaurant option as available early.

VGC explicitly requires Roto-Glide for its **Jaune Sector 3** approach. GameWith recommends its **Rouge Sector 8** route after gaining Roto-Glide during Main Mission 16. Keep access requirements separate from Alpha spawn conditions. [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alpha_Pok%C3%A9mon) reports the general citywide Alpha unlock after Main Mission 05; this review establishes no rooftop-specific survey requirement.

Game8 reports daytime and sun at several roofs and day/night with sun at Magenta 8. Altema describes that Magenta 8 Dratini check in sun or rain. Preserve this disagreement. An observation or guide instruction is not proof of an exclusive time/weather restriction. No exact Alpha probability is adopted.
