## Source reliability / auditing rules

- **Official context:** [The Pokémon Company: Mega Dimension](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-gb/news/new_allies) gives broad features of Hyperspace and donuts, **not precise side quest rewards, levels, NPC interactions or routes**. Individual factual details below are primarily [Serebii's side-mission index and individual pages](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml), [Bulbapedia's game mission table](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)), [Dexerto's DLC mission list](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/pokemon-legends-z-a/all-hyperspace-side-missions-walkthrough-in-mega-dimension-dlc/) and [GameTrek mission walkthroughs](https://gmtreks.com/pokemon-z-a/). They are **independent, not official publisher statements**.
- **Coverage:** Read every `article#quest-181` through `article#quest-200`, including their unlock, start, reward and steps; the guide's standalone `side-missions.html#quest-N` targets are placeholders/not source-recovered, so a link is **not** proof the destination gives complete guidance. Exact map pin/roof and in-game message timing remain unverified.
- **Evidence labels:** **CORROBORATED** = two or more independent sources or a source and its game-objective transcript agree; **SINGLE-SOURCE** = record for later; **DISPUTED** = sources differ; **B GUIDE ERROR / OMISSION** = explicit bad text or missing practical detail within inspected reconstructed entry.
- **Preserve** correct B instructions: sequence, locations, passwords and earned Pokémon. Do not erase working player progress, checklist IDs or quest notes to rewrite all entries.
