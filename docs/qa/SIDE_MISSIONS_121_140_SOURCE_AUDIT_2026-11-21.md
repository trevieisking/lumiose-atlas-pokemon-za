## Method, sources and limitations

- **Official publisher scope:** [The Pokémon Company on Ansha, Hoopa and donuts](https://legends.pokemon.com/en-gb/news/new_allies) supports Hyperspace portals, donut effects and Hyperspace Berry mechanics; does not provide the twenty individual quest rewards or detailed locations. [Nintendo UK DLC support](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Purchases-Subscriptions/Games/Unable-to-Access-DLC-or-Upgrade-Pack-Pokemon-Legends-Z-A--2940166.html) establishes installed/purchased DLC verification, not exact side-mission triggers.
- **Community/specialist source details:** [Serebii mission index and individual side missions](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions.shtml), [Bulbapedia's transcription of in-game side mission objectives/rewards](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_(Legends:_Z-A)), [Bulbapedia in-game gift ledger](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_in-game_event_Pok%C3%A9mon_in_Pok%C3%A9mon_Legends:_Z-A), [Serebii TM list](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/tm.shtml) and [Dexerto Mega Dimension numbered table](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/pokemon-legends-z-a/all-hyperspace-side-missions-walkthrough-in-mega-dimension-dlc/). These are *not official Nintendo sources*. Where source conflicts appear, **leave unverified and do not change**.
- **Structural scope:** guide B source contains 203 unique side/EX articles, one each for #121–140 with matching `data-side-check` keys and standalone-route links. Current reward labels omit cash and quantities, and #136 contains an explicit uncertain placeholder. Audit **source cards**, not unrecovered standalone route pages or real-world map geometry.
- **Release boundary:** No source, compiled HTML, user-progress key, Supabase feedback, IONOS DNS, original Site, Stream Bandit TCG or PR merge changed in this audit. All edits are future, source-verified, narrowly tested tasks.
