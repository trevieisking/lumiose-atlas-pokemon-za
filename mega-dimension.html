Where the expansion starts

The preserved guide says to finish the base story, then check Hotel Z’s roof for Side Mission 120 after Main Mission 38. Ansha makes donuts that let Hoopa open time-limited distortions. Complete survey tasks and return to Corbeau as new missions unlock.

01 · Prepare

Choose berries and make a donut with useful effects and enough time.

02 · Explore

Enter the marked distortion, complete its displayed survey objectives and watch the timer.

03 · Advance

Report survey milestones to Corbeau and follow the next mission marker.

HYPERSPACE MISSION

Hyperspace Lumiose Survey No. 1

Read original route steps

Saved mission instructions

  1. After Side Mission 120, go to the Rust Syndicate office when Corbeau calls. Vinnie updates the city map so it can detect distortions; Ansha makes donuts to let Hoopa open them.
  2. Enter the marked pockets, complete their displayed survey tasks and report to Corbeau. Then battle Korrina when she joins the investigation.
  3. Use the new Hyper Berries for the next portal. Rogue Mega Absol Z is Dark/Ghost, so Fighting moves fail; dodge its repeated Shadow Claw attack, defeat it and catch it for Absolite Z.

12
HYPERSPACE MISSION

The Greatest Gift

Read original route steps

Saved mission instructions

  1. After Darkrai, meet Corbeau and learn about the ruby and sapphire distortions. Get the Hoennian Salt and donut recipes, then complete Missions 13 and 14 to catch Groudon and Kyogre.
  2. Return to Ansha’s Rayquaza lead. Earn 100,000 Survey Points, make the Delta Old-Fashioned with at least 120 Sweet, 40 Spicy, 340 Sour, 40 Bitter and 390 Fresh, and enter the portal above Hotel Z.
  3. Rayquaza has regular and Mega phases. Dodge its Dragon Ascent and twister sequence; attacking the summoned twister can interrupt its dive. Catch Rayquaza after the Mega phase, then show it to Ansha at Hotel Z.

HYPERSPACE MISSION

A Ruby-Red Legend

Read original route steps

Saved mission instructions

  1. Use the ruby distortion at Jaune Sector 11. Make the Omega Old-Fashioned with at least 260 Sweet, 160 Spicy, 160 Sour, 20 Bitter and 260 Fresh.
  2. Defeat Groudon and then Primal Groudon. Its Primal weather shuts off Water moves; dodge the shifting lava and use another counter.
  3. Catch Groudon and receive the Red Orb; complete the sapphire branch before returning to the Rayquaza route.

HYPERSPACE MISSION

A Sapphire-Blue Legend

Read original route steps

Saved mission instructions

  1. Use the sapphire distortion in Wild Zone 5. Make the Alpha Old-Fashioned with at least 50 Sweet, 50 Spicy, 210 Sour, 180 Bitter and 370 Fresh.
  2. Defeat Kyogre and then Primal Kyogre. Dodge the repeated Origin Pulse beams; use Electric or Grass damage against its Water typing.
  3. Catch Kyogre and receive the Blue Orb. With both legendary branches complete, return to Mission 12 for Rayquaza and the final survey.
