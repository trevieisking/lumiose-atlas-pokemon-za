Main Mission 01 · Arrival

Get Your Travel Bag Back

Story

After this mission: Rank Z · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 20. Obedience limits

Main Mission 03 · Arrival

A New Life in Lumiose City

Story

Recommended order: Daylight: learn catching in Wild Zone 1 and register five species. Next chapter: enter the night Battle Zone for your first Challenger’s Ticket.

After this mission: Rank Z · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 20. Obedience limits

Purpose: learn the daily gameplay loop and unlock research. Catch different species rather than duplicates to reach five registered Pokémon, report to Mable and return to the hotel. The next mission introduces the Royale promotion objective.

  1. Meet your teammate in the lobby and battle outside Hotel Z.
  2. Visit the Pokémon Center opposite Gare de Lumiose and install the Pokédex app.
  3. Before the next marker, collect the glowing Potion, Poké Ball and Antidote nearby. Enter Wild Zone 1.
  4. Catch Fletchling and Bunnelby, register five species, claim Mable’s research reward, then return to Hotel Z.
Practical checks

Catch several different species, not just several copies of one Pokémon. Keep a Mareep from Zone 1 if you want an early Electric answer for Zach.

Story

Recommended order: Complete Emma’s three required requests, then visit the newly opened Wild Zones 2–6 in numbered order. Return later for unreachable roofs. The next promotion ticket is earned at night in Mission 6.

After this mission: Rank Y · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 25. Obedience limits

Main Mission 10 · The Royale

The Secrets of Mega Evolution

Story

Recommended order: Night: earn the ticket and beat Vinnie for Rank F. Daylight: check Wild Zones 7–10. Then take the Slowbro, Camerupt and Victreebel boss cards (Missions 11–13) in any order; report back after all three.

After beating Vinnie: Rank F · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 60 (was Rank V, through Lv. 40). Obedience limits

  1. The next morning, battle your teammate to practise Mega Evolving Absol. Go to Quasartico Inc.; defeat the two security guards and meet Vinnie.
  2. Earn a Challenger’s Ticket in the nighttime Battle Zone, then challenge Vinnie. Beat his Houndoom, Sharpedo and Buneary (Lv. 30) and Mega Drampa (Lv. 32) to jump from Rank V to Rank F.
  3. AZ gives you your starter’s Mega Stone. Take the Slowbro, Camerupt and Victreebel reports; Wild Zones 7–10 open now, so use daylight to visit them before the next three boss cards.
  4. Follow Missions 11–13 for the three Rogue Mega cases in any order. Once all three are done, return to Hotel Z, battle Zygarde, receive the mysterious Pebble and meet L.
Practical checks

Vinnie’s last Pokémon is Mega Drampa; keep an Ice, Fighting or Fairy answer after handling Houndoom, Sharpedo and Buneary. The Slowbro, Camerupt and Victreebel cases form a set. Check their separate mission cards and report back after all three are resolved.

A Rogue Mega Slowbro

Story

After this mission: Rank F · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 60. Obedience limits

Main Mission 14 · Mega mysteries

Reaching Rank E

Story

Recommended order: Night: secure the ticket. Then follow Naveen’s password, Canari’s contest, Tarragon’s battle and the Rank E promotion in the exact order below.

After beating Canari: Rank E · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 65 (was Rank F, through Lv. 60). Obedience limits

  1. Outside Hotel Z, beat Naveen (Canari’s top fan) to get his help. Earn a Challenger’s Ticket in the nighttime Battle Zones, then speak to Naveen again.
  2. Meet Naveen near the Electrical Substation in Magenta District. He gives you the admission code. Speak to the guard before entering and choose the three words separately: “Let’s” → “go” → “DYN4MO!” This is the entry password, not the first true/false question.
  3. Inside the contest, stand on ✓ for True (falling-block games), × for False (macarons), and ✓ for True (Electric-type Pokémon); confirm each choice.
  4. For round two, go to Café Gallant in Magenta Sector 1. Speak to the waiter; Naveen gives you the Super Lumiose Galette, a Key Item for Eelektross. Return to the Electrical Substation and present it at the contest stage.
  5. Defeat fellow fan Mani (Helioptile Lv. 34, Emolga Lv. 34, Raichu Lv. 35) for the right to answer the final question. Choose “Her grandpa” (option 4) for the childhood laptop, then ask for a promotion match.
  6. Follow the marker to Racine Construction in Vert Sector 6. Go inside and defeat Tarragon, Canari’s grandfather (Hippowdon Lv. 36, Diggersby Lv. 36, Mega Excadrill Lv. 37). This battle is required before Canari.
  7. Investigate the small room in the garage and talk to Canari. Win her Rank E promotion match against Heliolisk Lv. 37, Ampharos Lv. 38, Stunfisk Lv. 38 and Mega Eelektross Lv. 39.
Practical checks

The admission password, true/false round, galette errand, Mani battle, grandpa answer, Tarragon battle and Canari fight happen in that order. Heal between the last two battles.

Promotion to Rank E against Canari. See the opponent and counter plan. See battle details.

Main Mission 19 · Syndicate

Reaching Rank D

Story

Recommended order: A ticket alone does not start this promotion: find Gwynn and win her battle first. Then return to Ivor for Rank D.

After beating Ivor: Rank D · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 70 (was Rank E, through Lv. 65). Obedience limits

  1. Earn a Challenger’s Ticket in the nighttime Battle Zone. Head to the Justice Dojo southwest of Wild Zone 8 and accept Josee’s challenge. She uses Machoke and Medicham, both Lv. 43. Win to receive one Seed of Mastery. Josee (Josée in-game) explains that you can come back to the dojo and give her a Seed of Mastery to turn an eligible Pokémon move into a Plus Move.
  2. When Ivor arrives looking for Gwynn, go to the Looker Bureau and speak with Emma. Then meet Ivor outside City Hall in Jaune Sector 9. Emma asks you to question people nearby about Gwynn.
  3. Start with the man to your right outside City Hall who mentions someone missing, then the man farther along who says he’s found her. Continue behind City Hall toward the scaffolding and fountain. A boy nearby offers to help. For the lead you need, speak to the person beside the fountain whose speech bubble mentions the Fist of Justice. You’ll learn that Gwynn visits a café by Centrico Plaza with a friend.
  4. Fast-travel to Nouveau Café by Centrico Plaza and approach Ivor to trigger the scene. Choose “Possibly Canari”, then “We could visit Racine Construction”.
  5. Fast-travel to Racine Construction in Vert Sector 6. Pick up the Colorful Screw behind the reception desk if it’s still there, then go through the door on the right to find Ivor with Canari. Win the rematch against Heliolisk (Lv. 42), Ampharos (Lv. 43), Stunfisk (Lv. 43) and Mega Eelektross (Lv. 44) to hear Canari’s fountain clue. Choose Clawitzer when asked which Pokémon the clue points to.
  6. Go to the Clawitzer fountain’s courtyard in Bleu Sector 7, near Bleu Pokémon Center. Tell Emma you’re sure Gwynn came here and suggest stopping the water. Target the fountain with Ice Beam, then enter the Lumiose Sewers through the nearby door. If you need Ice Beam, collect TM013 in Wild Zone 12 and teach it to a compatible Pokémon.
  7. Follow the mission marker to the locked sewer gate and examine the lock. Follow Klefki and catch it with the Ultra Balls Emma gives you. Ivor opens the gate when Klefki’s key doesn’t fit. Continue until you catch up with Emma and Ivor, then follow Chandelure deeper into the sewers.
  8. At the apparent dead end, investigate all four suspicious spots: the pipes with valve wheels, the row of four machines along the wall, the electrical panels near the corner, and the pale crates in the stack. This triggers the scene that reveals the illusory wall. Walk straight through that wall and follow Chandelure down the next corridor.
  9. Speak to Gwynn and defeat her Banette (Lv. 43), Gourgeist (Lv. 44) and Mega Chandelure (Lv. 45). Then return to the Justice Dojo and defeat Ivor in the Rank D promotion match.
Practical checks

At City Hall, the person with an Aegislash looking for a Furfrou and the bystanders talking about “that girl”, Ivor or general trouble in Lumiose can be skipped. The Fist of Justice clue by the fountain advances the search. Bring Dark-type moves for Gwynn’s Ghost Pokémon, then heal and prepare for Ivor’s Fighting team before the promotion.

Promotion to Rank D against Ivor. See the opponent and counter plan. See battle details.

Main Mission 22 · Syndicate

A Rogue Mega Barbaracle

Story

After this mission: Rank D · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 70. Obedience limits

  1. Reach Barbaracle at the Bleu District case marker.
  2. Circle out of its charge and use a Grass move against its 4× Grass weakness.
  3. Collect its Mega Stone and resume the linked Syndicate reports.
