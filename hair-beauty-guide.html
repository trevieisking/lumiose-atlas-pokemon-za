LUMIOSE STYLE / BEAUTY

Find your signature look.

Give your trainer a makeover without changing their battle build. Find a salon for hair, or use the Outfits & Looks menu for facial details. All hairstyles are available to any trainer.

Choose a hairstyle

At the salon, choose the cut, fringe (if compatible) and colour together before confirming. Colour blocking gives a two-tone division; balayage blends a second colour through the hair. Choose one of the two colouring methods, not both.

14 standard cuts

Hi-top fade · Undercut pixie · Flyaway short cut · Slicked back · Simple short hair · Half-up chignon · Simple bob · Low chignon · Asymmetrical bob · Ponytail · Braids · Long and wavy · Side ponytail · Pigtails.

Fringe and eyebrows

Middle part, side part, extra long and blunt fringe are available on compatible cuts. Eyebrows can be thick, average or thin; the colour can be customised separately.

Side Mission 012 — The Many Flowers of Flabébé

After Main Mission 6, speak to Vivace outside Coiffure Clips. Show five Flabébé, one of each flower colour: red, yellow, orange, blue and white. Each flower unlocks its corresponding hair colour (the yellow form unlocks Lemon yellow); completing the set unlocks aqua, passion pink and purple. Reward: ₽1,500 and 1 Rare Candy, plus eight colours.

Side Mission 051 — Floette Frolicking with Flowers

After completing 012 and reaching the Canari battle milestone in Main Mission 19, return to Vivace on South Boulevard. Vivace brings all five opposing Floette. You do not need to catch or evolve any Floette to unlock the pastel dyes. See the battle tips. Unlock lime, turquoise, gray, pale pink, pale blue, cream, lavender, peach and mint. Reward: ₽2,500 and 2 Rare Candies, plus nine colours.

Where to find all five Flabébé colours

Catch one Flabébé of each flower colour before returning to Vivace for Side Mission 012. These are reported encounter areas, not measured map pins. Check the flower colour before catching. For the separate Alpha collection, see the Alpha Flabébé roof and portal routes.

Red
Start in the middle or eastern part of Wild Zone 3.
Yellow
Search the grass along Vert Street, between Vert Plaza and Prism Tower. Start at Nouveau Café or Wild Zone 2.
White
Look around the statues in the courtyard in Vert Sector 3.
Orange
Search the roadside flowers on Bleu Street, south-west of Wild Zone 16, near the salon district.
Blue
Try the roofs south-west of the gazebo in Vert Sector 6; look for a ladder. Another reported option is the rooftop north of Wild Zone 7, reached by Holovator.

Once you have shown all five to Vivace, return later for Side Mission 051 and win her five-Floette battle to earn the pastel palette.

Blue and orange Floette for your collection

Blue Floette
Catch a blue Flabébé using the Vert Sector rooftop route above. Use the ladder on the south side of the building and evolve your Flabébé at Lv. 19 or higher. Catch a spare if you want to keep both forms.
Orange Floette
Once Main Mission 10 starts, look for Lv. 20–22 orange Floette in Wild Zone 7 at Magenta Plaza. Red Floette also appear there, so check the flower before throwing. You can also evolve an orange Flabébé at Lv. 19 or higher.

These are collecting routes, not requirements for Vivace’s pastel dye battle. Side Mission 012 asks to see Flabébé, not Floette.

Makeup, face and contacts

Press X, then − for Outfits & Looks; use ZR to switch to Looks. Adjust eye shape and contacts, eyelash style and colour, eyebrow shape and colour, lip colour, beauty spots, freckles or dark circles. You can change these without a paid hair appointment. Skin tone is fixed after initial character creation. A salon is required for haircut and hair colour changes.

Special contact lenses

Meet Arden on Vernal Avenue and win each successive battle while Mega Evolving the requested Pokémon. After Main Mission 10, Mission 030 (Mega Camerupt) gives Fired Up and Lovestruck. Mission 031 (Mega Sableye) gives Starry Eyes and Black Void. Mission 032 (Mega Medicham) gives Cyber and Poké Ball contacts. Each previous mission unlocks the next.

Style your Pokémon too

Mirte and Scyther offer grooming in Jaune Sector 6, near the Justice Dojo and Wild Zone 11. For an ordinary Furfrou, start in Wild Zone 11 (Lv. 30–32). For the fixed Lv. 58–61 Alpha, look on the bandstand in Wild Zone 19 in the daytime, after the early-morning window. Each trim costs ₽500. Natural Form plus nine trims make ten registered forms. The service may not have a permanent map icon, so use the district landmarks.

Mission 047 · Slash
Once Main Mission 19 begins, use Slash on the Substitute doll to unlock Heart, Star and Diamond.
Mission 062 · Razor Leaf
After 047 and returning to Hotel Z following the Rust Syndicate visit in Main Mission 20, demonstrate Razor Leaf for Debutante, Matron and Dandy.
Mission 087 · Psycho Cut
After finishing Side Mission 062, check in with Mirte once Main Mission 30 begins. Demonstrate Psycho Cut to unlock La Reine, Kabuki and Pharaoh.
Mission 103 · Facing the Furfrou League
After 087 and returning to Hotel Z following the Lysandre Labs visit in Main Mission 35, challenge the league with a party made entirely of Furfrou. The Zone 19 bandstand Alpha and five ordinary catches make a full team. Defeat its Elite Four and Champion for ₽30,000 and a Gold Bottle Cap; this is a battle reward, not another trim.

Show off your new look

Use the Rotom Phone camera to take photos around Lumiose. At benches, pose with up to six Pokémon from your team. Make your Z-A Royale profile picture your own with poses, expressions and filters.
