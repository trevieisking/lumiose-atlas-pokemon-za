What we can and cannot show yet

📷 Direct photographic evidence

Original owner photographs support many lift fronts, map close-ups and some rooftop arrivals. Photographs load only if their real JPG file is already present in this GitHub repository. Unknown images are labelled pending, not replaced with generated pictures.

🏠 Building interiors

A building can contain corridors, courtyards, stairs, hidden walls and different floors. An exterior map cannot tell us the whole interior. The route cards preserve the known sequence of spaces without drawing fictional rooms, floorplans or doors.

🧭 Navigation limits

No GPS-like live position comes from the Switch game. Choose your starting landmark manually. A rotated bird's-eye map is still 2D, not a full 3D fly-through. Dynamic DLC distortions and unverified ladders are never pinned permanently.
