THE STORY LADDERZ → A10 promotion matches

Vinnie’s special battle changes the climb: Rank V jumps directly to F. This is the single-player story Royale; online Ranked Battles are a different mode.

How to earn the next promotion fight

At night, enter an active Battle Zone. Defeat roaming Trainers to collect Ticket Points; once you have a Challenger’s Ticket, follow the story marker to the promotion opponent. Heal between fights, choose a lead using type matchups, and keep a second answer available for the opponent’s final Pokémon.

Follow the in-game marker: sometimes the promotion is interrupted by a puzzle, investigation or another Trainer battle. Complete those objectives before returning to the rank opponent.

Move availability: the suggested moves below come from the owner's guide and are examples, not a promise that your Pokémon already knows them. Check the current move menu or teach a compatible TM before entering the battle.

MAIN MISSION 14 / RANK E

Canari Quiz Whiz: every answer in order

Before the quiz: battle Canari’s top fan Naveen outside Hotel Z, earn the nighttime Challenger’s Ticket and meet him again near the Electrical Substation. Tell the guard the entry words separately: Let’s → go → DYN4MO! That is the entry password, not the first True/False answer.

ROUND ONE

True / False / True

  1. Falling-block games: ✓ True
  2. Macarons: × False
  3. Electric-type Pokémon: ✓ True

Stand in the indicated answer area and confirm each choice.

ROUND TWO

Super Lumiose Galette

Go to Café Gallant in Magenta Sector 1. Speak to the waiter; Naveen gives you the special galette. Return to the Electrical Substation and present it at the stage for Eelektross.

FINAL ROUND

Defeat Mani, then answer

Battle Mani before the childhood laptop question. Choose “Her grandpa” (option 4), then request the promotion match.

REQUIRED FOLLOW-UP

Racine Construction

Go to Vert Sector 6 and defeat Tarragon, Canari’s grandfather. Inspect the small garage room, then battle Canari for Rank E. Open the exact Mission 14 sequence.

WHEN THE RANK MARKER WAITS

Story battles around the ladder

BEFORE CANARI

Tarragon’s Ground team

After Naveen and the quiz, Tarragon uses Hippowdon, Diggersby and Mega Excadrill. Bring Water or Grass for the first two; a Water move also works against Ground/Steel Excadrill. Follow Mission 14.

BEFORE IVOR

Find Gwynn in the sewers

Investigate the fountain in Bleu Sector 7, follow the sewer clues and battle Gwynn’s Banette, Gourgeist and Mega Chandelure. Dark coverage is useful; Water or Ground hits Chandelure’s Fire side. See all nine investigation steps.

BEFORE JACINTHE

Hotel tournament rematches

The hotel tournament includes stronger rematches with Canari, Ivor and Corbeau. Heal and rotate Electric/Ground, Flying/Fairy and Psychic/Ground counters before Jacinthe’s Fairy team. Open Mission 30.
