Restaurants & full-course battles

Each restaurant has its associated side mission. Prepare the whole party before the course; do not rely on healing between rounds. The opening missions are free. For a repeat course, bring the entry fee up front; you get the listed item rewards only after clearing the course. For outfit money, compare treasure sale prices and the Gold Canari Plush bonus. For Mable’s Trainer-win research, choose Le Nah, Le Yeah or Le Wow instead of Sushi High Roller.

Restaurant Le Nah

3 courses · ₽3,000

Free opening course: Side Mission 29

First clear: ₽300, TM085 Substitute, Protein ×2 and Iron ×2.

Repeat reward: 5 Tiny Mushrooms; 3 Exp. Candy S

The lowest entry fee for a course with three Trainer wins.

Restaurant Le Yeah

5 courses · ₽15,000

Free opening course: Side Mission 60

First clear: ₽1,000, TM094 Whirlwind, Calcium ×3 and Zinc ×3.

Repeat reward: 30 Tiny Mushrooms; 4 Exp. Candy M

Selling the 30 Tiny Mushrooms returns ₽7,500 of the fee before battle winnings.

Sushi High Roller

5 courses; three opposing Pokémon at once · ₽30,000

Free opening course: Side Mission 73

First clear: ₽1,500, and 10 each of Qualot, Grepa and Kelpsy Berries.

Repeat reward: 2 Big Nuggets; 1 Seed of Mastery. These encounters do not count toward Mable's Trainer-battle wins.

Sell two Big Nuggets for ₽40,000 total: ₽10,000 net after entry, plus the Seed of Mastery.

Restaurant Le Wow

10 courses · ₽100,000

Free opening course: Side Mission 94

First clear: ₽2,000, TM045 Knock Off, HP Up ×5 and Carbos ×5.

Repeat reward: 10 Pearl Strings; 10 Exp. Candy L.

Sell ten Pearl Strings for ₽100,000 total to recover the entry fee. Net cash then equals the battle winnings: ₽26,400 without a bonus, or ₽39,600 with a fully upgraded Gold Canari Plush.

Facing the Furfrou League (Side Mission 103) visits these same four restaurants, but uses separate mission opponents and rewards. See the catching and grooming route before building your team.

Useful people & services

Lost Poké Ball collectors

Outside the nine regular Pokémon Centers

Speak to the volunteer to collect eligible balls that missed a target. Free; failed captures are different. Excess above bag capacity is held for later.

Friendship checker

Café Pokémon-Amie, Autumnal Avenue

Complete Side Mission 25, Trubblesome Patrons. Speak to the person with Roselia. Feedback corresponds to friendship ranges 0; 1–29; 30–79; 80–129; 130–179; 180–219; 220–254; 255.

EV removal

Rust Syndicate reception, Bleu District

Spend five Mega Shards to reset one Pokémon's effort values at reception. The story takes you to the office in Main Mission 20. If the reset is not available on that visit, return after completing Main Mission 25.

Hyper Training

Justice Dojo

After completing Main Mission 19 (Reaching Rank D), speak to the attendant on the right inside Justice Dojo. Bring a Lv. 50 or higher Pokémon. A Bottle Cap treats one stat as IV 31; a Gold Bottle Cap does this for all six. The Pokémon’s original IVs do not change.

IV Judge

Boxes after the main-story credits

After the credits at the end of Main Mission 37, open Boxes, highlight a Pokémon and press the right stick. If Judge is still missing, visit a Pokémon Center and try Boxes again. This is separate from Hyper Training. Individual IVs: 31 Best, 30 Fantastic, 26–29 Very Good, 16–25 Pretty Good, 1–15 Decent, 0 No Good. IV total 151–186: Amazing stats; 121–150: Great stats; 91–120: Good stats; 0–90: OK stats.

Plus Move mastery

Josée, Justice Dojo

After defeating Josée in Main Mission 19, speak to her at Justice Dojo. Moves a Pokémon learns by levelling can master naturally. For a move it learns only by TM, use a Seed of Mastery for permanent mastery. When locked on and you have enough Mega Power, press + to select a mastered Plus Move. While Mega Evolved, all moves are already treated as Plus Moves.

Special Scan

Rust Syndicate

After Side Mission 188, earn 25,000 Hyperspace Survey Points per special five-star portal scan. When Corbeau calls, return to his office and speak to Philippe. Results may include Legendary Pokémon, battle zones or rare species; no particular species or permanent map marker is guaranteed.

Birthday registration

Pokémon Center clerk

After your first Looker Bureau visit, the next clerk may offer registration. The visual celebration details remain to be verified.

Pokémon training academy

Friendship

Friendship is capped at 255. Friendship evolutions need 160 plus any species-specific condition. At 255, the base critical-hit chance doubles from 1/24 to 1/12. Most training methods stop at 160. Each café drink adds 30 friendship to the active Pokémon; spending at least 10 seconds on a bench adds 10 to every party Pokémon. Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa and Tamato Berries also add 1 friendship above 160. Select the Pokémon you want to bond with before ordering.

Find the Friendship Checker · Pokédex

IVs, EVs and natures

IVs are individual stat values (0–31); EVs are training investment. Use the Judge, dojo and EV reset service above. Mints change effective nature stat modifiers. You can invest up to 252 EVs in one stat and 510 overall. An Alpha that already has 252 HP EVs won’t gain more HP EVs from vitamins. Decide whether to keep that investment before paying for a full reset. An EV-reducing berry lowers only its matching stat.

Find training services

Size and Alpha status

Ordinary size classes are XS, S, M, L and XL. Alphas are larger than normal XL specimens, although their displayed category is XL. Check mission-specific size requirements separately.

Alpha catalogue

Species-specific passive effects

Z-A does not use the conventional Ability system. Aegislash changes form via its moves; Mimikyu can block its first hit when undamaged; Morpeko changes with Aura Wheel; Dondozo and Tatsugiri interact when paired. Primal and Rogue boss effects should not be assumed to work for a captured Pokémon.

Multiplayer, trading & battle music

Play with friends

Open Link Play after the Mable Rewards feature becomes available. Use nearby play for local players, or faraway play for online players. Online play requires the supported software version, internet and Nintendo Switch Online; use compatible versions for local play.

  1. For a private battle, create a room in the selected mode; share the room code privately.
  2. Friends join in the matching mode using the generated passcode.
  3. The host chooses the rules; select a battle team and confirm readiness. Up to four players can battle.
  4. For Link Trade, both participants enter the same eight-digit trade code, check the offered Pokémon and confirm the exchange. Do not share private codes publicly.

Repeatable challenges

Rogue Mega Simulator

After the main story and Yveltal, speak to Vinnie at Quasartico. The level-70 rematches record completion time and previous best. Published reward: 3 Exp. Candy M per successful rematch.

Existing boss strategies

Rogue Mega Rush

Talk to Vinnie at Quasartico after clearing every Rogue Mega encounter. If the option is still missing after Rayquaza, complete Side Mission EX3: Raging Lightning (Zeraora) and check again. Choose one party for all 27 Lv. 100 fights. You cannot change the party roster or use bag items. You get three full-party heals between fights, so bring moves such as Giga Drain or Synthesis to save them for later. The game records bosses defeated and elapsed time, but there is no time limit. A full clear earns the Mega Rush Master title; the Simulator’s rematch candy payout does not apply here.

Base-game boss strategies · DLC encounters

Defenseless Dodger

After beating Canari and Ivor in Hyperspace Mission 9, talk to Canari inside Racine Construction. Dodge around the edge of the arena for 30 seconds without using Pokémon or fighting back. The first clear gives ₽3,400, TM130 Charge Beam and the Defenseless Dodger title. You can replay for a better record; repeat payouts are unverified.

Side Mission 173

Time, weather & trainer health

Day and night

Day: 04:00–20:00; night: 20:00–04:00. An in-game hour is approximately two real minutes. Rest at benches to skip to the next period and refresh spawns. Night opens Battle Zones; locations and encounters are conditional, not permanent pins.

Weather

Conditions include standard weather, overcast, intense sun, rain, fog and rainbow. Intense sun favours Fire and weakens Water; rain favours Water and weakens Fire. Check rainy Goomy/Sliggoo areas and sunny Riolu/Helioptile areas in their species guides; this is not a universal spawn rule.

Pokédex

Trainer health

Enemy attacks can hurt the trainer. Recover when safe and no longer targeted. Blacking out returns you to your last Pokémon Center; a Rogue Mega battle must restart. Green Canari Plush increases the maximum from 100 to 150, 200 and 250 as upgraded.
