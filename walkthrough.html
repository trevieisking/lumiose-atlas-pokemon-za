Reaching Rank E

⌖ Magenta Electrical Substation → Café Gallant → Vert Sector 6 Racine Construction

Check progress in the main walkthrough
Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. Outside Hotel Z, beat Naveen (Canari’s top fan) to get his help. Earn a Challenger’s Ticket in the nighttime Battle Zones, then speak to Naveen again.
  2. Meet Naveen near the Electrical Substation in Magenta District. He gives you the admission code. Speak to the guard before entering and choose the three words separately: “Let’s” → “go” → “DYN4MO!” This is the entry password, not the first true/false question.
  3. Inside the contest, stand on ✓ for True (falling-block games), × for False (macarons), and ✓ for True (Electric-type Pokémon); confirm each choice.
  4. For round two, go to Café Gallant in Magenta Sector 1. Speak to the waiter; Naveen gives you the Super Lumiose Galette, a Key Item for Eelektross. Return to the Electrical Substation and present it at the contest stage.
  5. Defeat fellow fan Mani (Helioptile Lv. 34, Emolga Lv. 34, Raichu Lv. 35) for the right to answer the final question. Choose “Her grandpa” (option 4) for the childhood laptop, then ask for a promotion match.
  6. Follow the marker to Racine Construction in Vert Sector 6. Go inside and defeat Tarragon, Canari’s grandfather (Hippowdon Lv. 36, Diggersby Lv. 36, Mega Excadrill Lv. 37). This battle is required before Canari.
  7. Investigate the small room in the garage and talk to Canari. Win her Rank E promotion match against Heliolisk Lv. 37, Ampharos Lv. 38, Stunfisk Lv. 38 and Mega Eelektross Lv. 39.

Practical checks

  • The admission password, true/false round, galette errand, Mani battle, grandpa answer, Tarragon battle and Canari fight happen in that order. Heal between the last two battles.
Field tip

Bring Ground coverage for Electric threats, with a backup answer to her wider team.

Map cue: Magenta Electrical Substation → Café Gallant → Vert Sector 6 Racine Construction. District focus only. Follow the in-game marker for the exact arena.

Reaching Rank D

⌖ Citywide · Royale promotion

Check progress in the main walkthrough
Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. Earn a Challenger’s Ticket in the nighttime Battle Zone. Head to the Justice Dojo southwest of Wild Zone 8 and accept Josee’s challenge. She uses Machoke and Medicham, both Lv. 43. Win to receive one Seed of Mastery. Josee (Josée in-game) explains that you can come back to the dojo and give her a Seed of Mastery to turn an eligible Pokémon move into a Plus Move.
  2. When Ivor arrives looking for Gwynn, go to the Looker Bureau and speak with Emma. Then meet Ivor outside City Hall in Jaune Sector 9. Emma asks you to question people nearby about Gwynn.
  3. Start with the man to your right outside City Hall who mentions someone missing, then the man farther along who says he’s found her. Continue behind City Hall toward the scaffolding and fountain. A boy nearby offers to help. For the lead you need, speak to the person beside the fountain whose speech bubble mentions the Fist of Justice. You’ll learn that Gwynn visits a café by Centrico Plaza with a friend.
  4. Fast-travel to Nouveau Café by Centrico Plaza and approach Ivor to trigger the scene. Choose “Possibly Canari”, then “We could visit Racine Construction”.
  5. Fast-travel to Racine Construction in Vert Sector 6. Pick up the Colorful Screw behind the reception desk if it’s still there, then go through the door on the right to find Ivor with Canari. Win the rematch against Heliolisk (Lv. 42), Ampharos (Lv. 43), Stunfisk (Lv. 43) and Mega Eelektross (Lv. 44) to hear Canari’s fountain clue. Choose Clawitzer when asked which Pokémon the clue points to.
  6. Go to the Clawitzer fountain’s courtyard in Bleu Sector 7, near Bleu Pokémon Center. Tell Emma you’re sure Gwynn came here and suggest stopping the water. Target the fountain with Ice Beam, then enter the Lumiose Sewers through the nearby door. If you need Ice Beam, collect TM013 in Wild Zone 12 and teach it to a compatible Pokémon.
  7. Follow the mission marker to the locked sewer gate and examine the lock. Follow Klefki and catch it with the Ultra Balls Emma gives you. Ivor opens the gate when Klefki’s key doesn’t fit. Continue until you catch up with Emma and Ivor, then follow Chandelure deeper into the sewers.
  8. At the apparent dead end, investigate all four suspicious spots: the pipes with valve wheels, the row of four machines along the wall, the electrical panels near the corner, and the pale crates in the stack. This triggers the scene that reveals the illusory wall. Walk straight through that wall and follow Chandelure down the next corridor.
  9. Speak to Gwynn and defeat her Banette (Lv. 43), Gourgeist (Lv. 44) and Mega Chandelure (Lv. 45). Then return to the Justice Dojo and defeat Ivor in the Rank D promotion match.

Practical checks

  • At City Hall, the person with an Aegislash looking for a Furfrou and the bystanders talking about “that girl”, Ivor or general trouble in Lumiose can be skipped. The Fist of Justice clue by the fountain advances the search.
  • Bring Dark-type moves for Gwynn’s Ghost Pokémon, then heal and prepare for Ivor’s Fighting team before the promotion.
Field tip

If Chandelure leads you to a dead end in the sewers, inspect all four marked spots and walk through the illusory wall.

Map cue: Citywide · Royale promotion. This objective moves across the city; follow its in-game marker.
