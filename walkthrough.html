What level will obey?

For wild Pokémon you catch, check their level when caught, not their current level. A Pokémon caught within the limit still obeys if you train it above the limit later. Check your current limit in the in-game Save screen.

The limit rises after you win the promotion match. Mission 09 leaves you at Rank V; defeating Vinnie during Mission 10 takes you straight to Rank F.

Get Your Travel Bag Back

⌖ South Boulevard · Gare de Lumiose

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After this mission: Rank Z · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 20. Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

Purpose: get your travel bag back and establish your first partner. Stay with the scripted chase; you are learning targeting and move cooldowns before the city opens up.

  1. As you leave Gare de Lumiose, Pancham steals your travel bag during your meeting with Taunie or Urbain.
  2. Follow Pancham through the nearby alley until its Trainer challenges you. Do not wander into the wider city yet.
  3. Choose your first partner from Chikorita, Tepig or Totodile; confirm when you are happy with your choice.
  4. Battle Backpacker Andi's level 4 Pancham. Hold ZL to target it and use your available moves once their real-time cooldowns allow.
  5. Win to recover the bag and earn ₽192. The next story event leads into Mission 02.

Practical checks

  • Pancham belongs to Andi: the opening fight is against its Trainer. Pick any of the three offered partners; the other two can be obtained later through the game or HOME.
Field tip

Your first partner is a preference pick; you can build a balanced team around any of the three.

Map cue: South Boulevard · Gare de Lumiose. This objective moves across the city; follow its in-game marker.

Escape from the Battle Zone

⌖ South Boulevard · Hotel Z

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After this mission: Rank Z · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 20. Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

Purpose: reach safety, not climb the Royale ladder yet. Your destination is Hotel Z; once checked in, the lift and Room 202 are the next objectives. Answer the morning knock to continue.

  1. As the city changes to night, follow Taunie or Urbain through the Battle Zone rather than challenging every passing Trainer.
  2. Defeat Brigitte the Waitress. She uses a level 4 Cleffa and a level 4 Bunnelby, and the battle awards ₽192.
  3. Continue through the left-hand archway, go right and catch up with your companion at the red boundary.
  4. Walk up to the red barrier and interact with it to exit, then turn right and follow the tunnel towards Hotel Z.
  5. Enter Hotel Z, talk to the front-desk attendant and receive the key to Room 202.
  6. Take the lift to 2F; Room 202 is beside the seating area. Enter, interact with the bed and sleep.
  7. When you hear a knock, answer the bedroom door to start Mission 03.

Practical checks

  • The battle-zone boundary marks the danger area. Follow your teammate to Hotel Z, use the lift and enter room 202 before looking for another objective.
Field tip

Watch the red boundary: battle zones are the nightly Royale areas.

03
Arrival · main story

A New Life in Lumiose City

⌖ Vert District · Wild Zone 1

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After this mission: Rank Z · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 20. Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

Purpose: learn the daily gameplay loop and unlock research. Catch different species rather than duplicates to reach five registered Pokémon, report to Mable and return to the hotel. The next mission introduces the Royale promotion objective.

  1. From Room 202, take the lift to the Hotel Z lobby. Meet Taunie or Urbain and Lida, receive the Team MZ outfit and learn about the Royale app.
  2. Learn to dash with B and dodge with Y. Outside the hotel, battle your teammate's two level 5 starter Pokémon to practise type matchups.
  3. Follow the marker towards the Pokémon Center facing Gare de Lumiose; use the ladder on the approach and speak at the counter to install the Pokédex app.
  4. Pick up the glowing Potion, Poké Ball and Antidote on the way to Wild Zone 1. Check its recommended level before entering.
  5. Practise catching Fletchling with a Poké Ball. Fight and weaken Bunnelby first, then catch it. Learn to crouch when approaching other wild Pokémon.
  6. Register five different Pokémon species in your Pokédex. Extra copies of the same species do not increase that species total.
  7. Leave through the west-side exit and enter the Pokémon Research Lab. Take the lift to 3F and speak with Mable.
  8. Use the X main menu to inspect Mable's Research and claim the first reward, TM004 Rock Smash. Keep checking her later levels as you explore.
  9. Return to Hotel Z to complete the mission. Your first night of Z-A Royale competition follows in Mission 04.

Practical checks

  • Catch several different species, not just several copies of one Pokémon. Keep a Mareep from Zone 1 if you want an early Electric answer for Zach.
Field tip

Use daylight to catch and train; the nightly Royale advances your rank.

Map cue: Vert District · Wild Zone 1. Tap a zone to see its full map and Alpha list.

Practical checks

  • Zach leads with Slowpoke and also has Pidgey and Pikachu. Grass helps against Slowpoke and Electric helps against Pidgey; heal before starting the promotion.

The Royale · main story

The City in the Shadow of Prism Tower

⌖ Across Lumiose · new Wild Zones 2–6

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After this mission: Rank Y · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 25. Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. After the Rank Y match, meet your teammate at Hotel Z and receive the Red Canari Plush, which increases experience gained.
  2. Take the rooftop shortcut: break the blocking Mega Crystals and collect 10 Mega Shards, climb the ladder and follow the rooftops. Use the narrow passage and safe drop when prompted.
  3. Stop near Wild Zone 2 if desired. An NPC gives you 10 Poké Balls, and TM027 Swift can be found nearby. Register Pokémon Centers for easier return travel.
  4. Meet your teammate by the Nouveau Café truck near Centrico Plaza and hear about the plans for Prism Tower, including Vinnie's involvement.
  5. On the approach to Wild Zone 3, fight Josée of the Fist of Justice: Machop level 10 and Meditite level 11. Winning pays ₽528.
  6. Explore Wild Zone 3 for new catches, notably Pikachu for a later trade. A nearby NPC gives three Great Balls; the stone building provides rooftop access to TM022 Aerial Ace.
  7. Follow the marker to Emma's Looker Bureau. She gives you three required requests: 001 A Big Ol' Bunnelby, 002 A Use for an Evolution Stone and 003 Sableye in the Cemetery.
  8. Complete the connected errands: find Trevelle's Alpha Bunnelby, trade a Pikachu from Wild Zone 3 to Tracie at Passage du Palais for Heracross, and recover Moire's Cherished Ring from Dormez Bien Cemetery in Wild Zone 4. You can tackle them in an order suited to your team.
  9. Return to Emma after all three are complete. Her level 15 Mareep is the final training battle; the win pays ₽900 and leads to Mission 06. Wild Zones 2–6 are available during this stage.

Practical checks

  • Requests 1–3 are required here. Request 1 introduces the Alpha encounter; Request 2 trades a Pikachu for Heracross; Request 3 sends you into the cemetery for a lost item.
  • Wild Zones 2–6 open now. Check the zone links and return later for any roofs you cannot yet reach.
Field tip

Activate nearby travel points and collect the new Wild Zone pickups as you travel.

Map cue: Across Lumiose · new Wild Zones 2–6. Tap a zone to see its full map and Alpha list.

Reaching Rank X

⌖ Nightly battle zone · location changes

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After beating Yvon: Rank X · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 30 (was Rank Y, through Lv. 25). Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. After learning about Mega Evolution at Emma's office, explore the city during daylight. Side requests 004–009 now become available, including the quests for Chespin, Fennekin and Froakie.
  2. When night arrives, use the map to enter the active Battle Zone. Fight Trainers to earn Ticket Points for your Challenger's Ticket.

Practical checks

  • Yvon uses two Fairy Pokémon, Spritzee and Swirlix, followed by Vivillon. Poison or Steel helps against the first two; Rock is especially strong against Bug/Flying Vivillon.
Field tip

Keep type coverage across your party rather than relying on one starter.

Reaching Rank W

⌖ Hotel Z and the nightly battle zone

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After beating Xavi: Rank W · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 35 (was Rank X, through Lv. 30). Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. After reaching Rank X, visit Hotel Z and head outside to speak with Naveen for the Team MZ training battle.
  2. Naveen's team consists of Spinarak level 16, Sableye level 16 and Scraggy level 17. Winning earns ₽952; keep a Fairy answer for Scraggy and Sableye. Normal- and Fighting-type moves do not affect Sableye.
  3. New side requests 010–016 are available during this stage. You can work through them during the day while raising your team.
  4. At night, enter the current Battle Zone and earn enough points for another Challenger's Ticket. Heal or replenish supplies before the final opponent.
  5. The promotion battle is near Hibernal Pokémon Center in the northeastern city. Grade-Schooler Xavi uses Venipede level 20, Roselia level 20, Kadabra level 21 and Furfrou level 21.
  6. Fire is useful against Venipede and Roselia; Dark/Ghost can handle Kadabra and Fighting can handle Furfrou. Keep your Fighting counter away from Kadabra's Psychic attacks.
  7. Defeat Xavi, gain ₽1,260 and reach Rank W. Continue to Mission 08.

Practical checks

  • Xavi uses Venipede, Roselia, Kadabra and Furfrou. Fire covers the first two, Dark helps against Kadabra and Fighting helps against Furfrou; protect the Fighting Pokémon from Kadabra.
Field tip

Top up healing items before entering the night zone.

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

Chase That Mysterious Pokémon

⌖ Follow the active story marker

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After this mission: Rank V · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 40. Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. After the Rank V promotion, look for the mysterious Zygarde following the story scene. Chase it through the marked streets and onto the rooftop; you cannot catch it here.
  2. Keep moving after Zygarde until you reach the distressed Absol. Emma and AZ arrive when its uncontrolled Mega Evolution begins.
  3. AZ provides Lucario to help with the Rogue Mega Absol fight. Use Lucario's attacks to dislodge Mega Power orbs, then move your Trainer to collect them.
  4. When the Mega gauge is ready, press the right control stick to Mega Evolve Lucario. Continue attacking during openings and collect more orbs if the Mega state ends.
  5. Dodge sideways when Absol sends out ranged attacks. When it begins a large red Dark Pulse ring, move away from the centre rather than standing within the area.
  6. Use Lucario's Aura Sphere or Rock Smash when Absol's attack animation gives you an opening. When it calms down, Absol joins your party and you obtain Absolite.
  7. Return to the story after the encounter. Mission 10 teaches you how to use your new Mega Evolution equipment.

Practical checks

  • Watch Absol’s expanding Dark Pulse and move the Trainer out of its area. Collect dropped Mega Power with the borrowed Lucario, then use its Fighting moves after you Mega Evolve.
Field tip

Collect Mega Power orbs, Mega Evolve Lucario and retreat from the large Dark Pulse before using Fighting moves.

Map cue: Follow the active story marker. This objective moves across the city; follow its in-game marker.

The Secrets of Mega Evolution

⌖ New Wild Zones 7–10 · citywide

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After beating Vinnie: Rank F · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 60 (was Rank V, through Lv. 40). Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. The next morning, put Absol in your party and give it Absolite. Talk to Taunie or Urbain outside Hotel Z and agree to test your Mega Evolution in a training battle.
  2. Your teammate uses two evolved level 25 starters and a level 26 Manectric that Mega Evolves. Build Mega Power, use Mega Absol and win the practice battle; the reward is ₽1,560.
  3. Wild Zones 7–10 are now open. New side requests 023–026 also become available, so collect Pokémon and useful items before the next major promotion if you need preparation.
  4. Head to Quasartico Inc. in the northwestern city. Two level 30 Dedenne block the route in a Double Battle; they are security Pokémon, not catchable wild encounters.
  5. Meet Vinnie, who changes your next Z-A Royale challenge into a jump from Rank V to Rank F. Return to night Battle Zones and earn a Challenger's Ticket.
  6. Face Vinnie's level 30 Houndoom, Sharpedo and Buneary, followed by his level 32 Mega Drampa. Water or Ground helps against Houndoom, Grass/Electric against Sharpedo, and Fighting or Fairy against Drampa's Normal/Dragon typing.
  7. After victory, enter Quasartico Inc. and speak with the staff member who exchanges collected Mega Shards for useful items and Mega Stones. Break pink crystals around the city to gather shards.
  8. Return via Nouveau Café and then to Hotel Z. AZ provides the Mega Stone matching your original Chikorita, Tepig or Totodile line.
  9. Three new Rogue Mega cases are available in any order: Mission 11 (Slowbro), 12 (Camerupt) and 13 (Victreebel). Clear them all before returning to the shared story objective.
  10. After all three are resolved, revisit Hotel Z for the Zygarde encounter, the mysterious Pebble and the introduction of L. Follow the next story marker to proceed towards Rank E.

Practical checks

  • Vinnie’s last Pokémon is Mega Drampa; keep an Ice, Fighting or Fairy answer after handling Houndoom, Sharpedo and Buneary.
  • The Slowbro, Camerupt and Victreebel cases form a set. Check their separate mission cards and report back after all three are resolved.
Field tip

AZ gives you your starter’s Mega Stone; the Slowbro, Camerupt and Victreebel cases can be tackled in any order.

Map cue: New Wild Zones 7–10 · citywide. Tap a zone to see its full map and Alpha list.

⌖ Magenta Electrical Substation → Café Gallant → Vert Sector 6 Racine Construction

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After beating Canari: Rank E · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 65 (was Rank F, through Lv. 60). Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. Outside Hotel Z, beat Naveen (Canari’s top fan) to get his help. Earn a Challenger’s Ticket in the nighttime Battle Zones, then speak to Naveen again.
  2. Meet Naveen near the Electrical Substation in Magenta District. He gives you the admission code. Speak to the guard before entering and choose the three words separately: “Let’s” → “go” → “DYN4MO!” This is the entry password, not the first true/false question.
  3. Inside the contest, stand on ✓ for True (falling-block games), × for False (macarons), and ✓ for True (Electric-type Pokémon); confirm each choice.
  4. For round two, go to Café Gallant in Magenta Sector 1. Speak to the waiter; Naveen gives you the Super Lumiose Galette, a Key Item for Eelektross. Return to the Electrical Substation and present it at the contest stage.
  5. Defeat fellow fan Mani (Helioptile Lv. 34, Emolga Lv. 34, Raichu Lv. 35) for the right to answer the final question. Choose “Her grandpa” (option 4) for the childhood laptop, then ask for a promotion match.
  6. Follow the marker to Racine Construction in Vert Sector 6. Go inside and defeat Tarragon, Canari’s grandfather (Hippowdon Lv. 36, Diggersby Lv. 36, Mega Excadrill Lv. 37). This battle is required before Canari.
  7. Investigate the small room in the garage and talk to Canari. Win her Rank E promotion match against Heliolisk Lv. 37, Ampharos Lv. 38, Stunfisk Lv. 38 and Mega Eelektross Lv. 39.

Practical checks

  • The admission password, true/false round, galette errand, Mani battle, grandpa answer, Tarragon battle and Canari fight happen in that order. Heal between the last two battles.
Field tip

Bring Ground coverage for Electric threats, with a backup answer to her wider team.

Map cue: Magenta Electrical Substation → Café Gallant → Vert Sector 6 Racine Construction. District focus only. Follow the in-game marker for the exact arena.

A Rogue Mega Beedrill

⌖ Vert District · Rogue Mega arena

Check progress in the main walkthrough

After this mission: Rank E · caught Pokémon obey through Lv. 65. Obedience limits

Read original mission steps and field tips

Your route

  1. Meet your teammate on the rooftops behind Hotel Z.
  2. Learn Roto-Glide and cross the rooftop gaps.
  3. Reach the Beedrill arena; revisit earlier roofs later for screws and TMs that needed the glide.

Practical checks

  • Use the newly taught Roto-Glide to cross the roof gaps. Beedrill is Bug/Poison; Fire, Flying, Psychic or Rock moves pressure it while you dodge its fast pin volley.
