LUMIOSE STYLE / CLOTHING

Dress for your own adventure.

Collect outfits, build a favourite look or simply browse. Every trainer can wear any of the available clothing styles. The city has 41 boutiques spread across five shopping arcades.

Unlock and change your outfit

Press X to open the main menu, then the minus (−) button for Outfits & Looks once unlocked early in the story. To buy clothes, continue the story until you have visited the Looker Bureau in Main Mission 5. Boutique Couture opens through Main Mission 26 (An Invitation from the SBC): defeat Asami using Mega Evolution during the story visit. Buying expensive clothing earlier will not unlock the door. A purchased piece becomes a wardrobe option: return to the menu to combine pieces rather than buying the same outfit again.

Choose an all-in-one outfit, or combine tops and bottoms. Other slots include headwear, eyewear, gloves, legwear, footwear, satchels and earrings. Claddia lends an ensemble, but return borrowed pieces before changing your look again.

Free and unlockable fashion

Team MZ clothing arrives through the story. The outfit menu is unlocked around the first hour of play. You can put together a new look using pieces you already own for no additional charge. If you own Mega Dimension, claim Holo-X and Holo-Y apparel from the Outfits & Looks menu after it opens; you need not wait until the DLC story. The later Hyperspatial Slugfest side missions 181–185 also award character-inspired clothing. See the hairstyle rewards and our side missions guide.

Fashion and battle supplies

Enjoy fashion at your own pace. An optional shopping habit: reserve whatever you need for healing before trying on expensive outfits. At published shop prices, 10 Max Potions and 5 ordinary Revives cost ₽16,000. This is an example budget, not a gameplay requirement. Check our medical supply prices or earn battle rewards before your next shopping trip.
