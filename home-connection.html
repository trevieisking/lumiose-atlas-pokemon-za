Bring your team
home.

Move Pokémon between your Z-A save and Pokémon HOME using Nintendo’s supported connection. Your first completed transfer unlocks three Alpha first partners, while dedicated HOME Pokédex completion leads to a Shiny Volcanion.

FIRST TRANSFER REWARD

Three Alpha first partners

Receiving the in-game Pokédex, installing HOME or linking an account is not enough. Nintendo’s official first-transfer reward requires moving a Pokémon caught in Legends: Z-A into HOME using the Switch version and saving that transfer. Both HOME apps must use the same Nintendo Account.

01

Set up both apps

Install Pokémon HOME on your Switch and mobile device. Use the same Nintendo Account for both and your Z-A save.

02

Deposit one Z-A Pokémon

Open the Switch version of HOME, select your Z-A save, move at least one Pokémon caught in Z-A into a HOME Box, and save.

03

Claim your gift on mobile

Open mobile HOME, go to Mystery Gifts and your Gift Box, and receive Alpha Chikorita, Alpha Tepig and Alpha Totodile.

04

Return to Lumiose

Open Switch HOME again, select your Z-A save and move the three gifts to your game Boxes. Save your changes.

Gift not showing? Check that the Pokémon originated from Z-A, that the deposit was saved and that the Switch and mobile versions use the same Nintendo Account. This reward is separate from choosing your first partner in Mission 1.

THREE-DEX COMPLETION / HOME GIFT

Shiny Volcanion

Complete the Lumiose Pokédex, Hyperspace Pokédex and Mega Evolution Pokédex in Pokémon HOME, confirm the completed records in HOME’s Games tab, and claim Shiny Volcanion through Mystery Gifts in the mobile HOME app.

The gift is once per Nintendo Account. Trainers who completed the records before its release remain eligible. The Hyperspace Pokédex requires the Mega Dimension expansion. Shiny Volcanion is separate from the ordinary Volcanion obtained during the DLC.

Important: completing the Pokédex inside Z-A alone does not automatically prove that HOME has registered the required Z-A-origin Pokémon and Mega Evolution records. Check the completion indicators in HOME itself. Not every event-exclusive entry shown in the game’s 364-species list is required for HOME’s completion reward.

CHECK BEFORE MOVING

What can travel into Z-A?

◎

Species eligibility

Only Pokémon represented in Z-A’s available species roster can be transferred into this game. The owner’s PDF lists 232 Lumiose and 132 Hyperspace species; the latter require the Mega Dimension content to appear in that expansion.

Always check the individual Pokémon’s “Games it can travel to” label in HOME. Its origin and form can restrict compatibility even when the species is listed below.

↛

One-way rule for older games

A Pokémon caught in Z-A cannot be sent to earlier titles. More importantly, if you transfer a Pokémon from an older game into Z-A, you cannot later send that individual Pokémon back to previous games.

Think carefully before moving a valued legacy Pokémon from your older collection. Official Pokémon guidance confirms this restriction.

Some forms need transfers or distributions

The 364 entries below are species, not a catalogue of every available form. Your original guide identifies some Vivillon patterns, Original Color Magearna and Roaming Form Gimmighoul as special cases requiring HOME or distributions. The regular Magearna and Chest Form Gimmighoul have their own Z-A routes. A Spewpa Mystery Gift previously provided additional Vivillon patterns. Separately, the Poké Ball Pattern Vivillon gift can be claimed in Legends: Z-A through Mystery Gift → Get via Internet until 26 February 2027 at 01:59 UTC. See Online Rewards for current gifts.

For compatibility, check each Pokémon individually in HOME. The list is a species-level planning aid, not a guarantee that every form or older-game Pokémon can move to Z-A.
