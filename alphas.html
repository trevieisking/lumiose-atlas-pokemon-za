Big targets. Better plans.

How to hunt Alphas safely

01 / UNLOCK

Finish “A Big Ol’ Bunnelby”

During Main Mission 5, complete Side Mission 1. This introduces the Alpha Pokémon system. Completing a Wild Zone’s Pokédex survey can unlock extra random Alpha chances there.

02 / FIND

Check level and time of day

Each zone card below lists fixed Alpha targets and their conditions. Visit a Pokémon Center in person to register it before relying on return fast travel. Use the map section to choose the zone.

03 / CATCH

Approach with a capture plan

Carry appropriate Balls and healing items. Approach from cover where possible, or battle and weaken the Alpha while dodging its moves. Be especially careful with the one-time Mission 40 encounters: catch rather than defeat if you want them.

04 / RECORD

Check the rest of the fieldbook

Fixed city Alphas, fossils, HOME gifts and Hyperspace Alpha Power use different methods. The cards below do not represent the entire Alpha system, so check the sections after Zone 20.

Why catch an Alpha Pokémon?

Beyond their size and red eyes, caught Alphas have practical training advantages. These are properties of the Pokémon you catch, not a promise that any specific wild encounter will spawn as an Alpha.

BUILT-IN STATS

At least three perfect IVs

Every Alpha has at least three Individual Values (IVs) at 31, chosen among its six stats. The remaining IVs are not guaranteed perfect. This can reduce the amount of stat optimisation you need later.

TRAINING START

252 HP Effort Values

A captured Alpha already has 252 HP EVs, the maximum allocation for that stat. It can be useful immediately, but that fixed starting allocation might not match every competitive build; check its training before adding more EVs.

SPECIAL MOVE

A mastered TM move

Alphas arrive with a species-specific move normally learned through a TM, already mastered as a Plus Move. Watch for that move when battling one: its attacks may be more varied than you expect.

HUNTING REWARDS

Experience and mastery items

Defeating or catching Alphas can award Exp. Candy and Seeds of Mastery, depending on the encounter. The larger XL-sized body and Alpha marking also remain useful collection features. Do not assume a specific item drop or additional wild battle-strength bonus after capture.

Source cross-check: Serebii and Bulbapedia (English), GameWith and Game8 (Japanese). The 31-IV and 252-HP-EV guarantees concern captured Alpha traits; they are not the chance of a random city Alpha appearing.

BEYOND THE WILD ZONES

Other Alpha routes worth checking

City encounters fall into two groups: fixed Alpha targets and ordinary habitats that can sometimes produce an Alpha. The locations below are researched search routes, not guaranteed Alpha spawns or new map pins.

FIXED CITY TARGETS

Search the districts

District names are starting areas, not exact rooftop, ladder or encounter coordinates.

CITY ROOFTOPS / CHANCE ENCOUNTER

Find Alpha Riolu above Lumiose

Riolu is found on city rooftops rather than at a fixed Wild Zone Alpha target. Guides document random Alpha Riolu around Lv. 25–29 at suitable rooftop encounters. Look in sunny daytime (including morning or afternoon); ordinary Riolu may appear instead. An Alpha is not guaranteed, and no individual spawn probability is verified.

Start at Rouge Sector 4: From the Looker Bureau, head towards the shopping passage near Wild Zone 3. The outside wall of the shopping-centre building has a ladder leading to the rooftop Riolu search spot. This route does not require Roto-Glide.

Other documented roofs: Bleu Sector 5 (follow the waterway access and climb to the roof), Vert Sector 8 (northeast of Hotel Z), and Magenta Sector 9 (roofs near Quasartico Inc., accessed via scaffolding and ladders). Some alternative roof crossings need Roto-Glide, but it is not a universal unlock requirement.

Check the weather, look for either ordinary or Alpha Riolu, and revisit after the encounter changes if necessary. These are search-area directions, not exact verified map coordinates.

CITY COURTYARDS & ROOFTOPS / CHANCE ENCOUNTERS

Ralts, Larvitar and Dedenne can also appear as Alphas

Ralts (roughly Lv. 20–24 Alpha): Check the inner courtyards in Vert Sector 4, Bleu Sector 7 and Magenta Sector 7 during sunny daytime. The Bleu 7 spot can be approached from the Southside Street Holovator and then reached by dropping into the enclosed courtyard. These are reported random, not fixed, Alpha variants.

Larvitar (roughly Lv. 30–34 city Alpha): Check rooftop habitats in Rouge Sector 7, Bleu Sector 7 and Jaune Sector 6. Some routes require Roto-Glide or an unlocked Holovator. A separate random Alpha Larvitar can appear on a Wild Zone 15 rooftop after that zone’s Pokédex survey is complete; do not confuse the two mechanisms.

Dedenne (around Lv. 27 city Alpha): One documented search is the roof southwest of Quasartico Inc., reached from a ladder in the Magenta Sector 7 courtyard; a guide reports a night/sunny Alpha there. Another reported rooftop is Jaune Sector 11, listed for sunny day or night. Wild Zone 20’s rotating Alpha Dedenne is a separate encounter.

For all three, the normal habitat is not proof of a guaranteed Alpha; source-specific time and weather reports do not establish universal rules. No new Alpha pins, estimated coordinates or invented access points have been added.

CITY ROOFTOPS / CHANCE ENCOUNTER

Find Alpha Dratini above Lumiose

Look for Alpha Dratini (Lv. 38–39) on city rooftops, not in the Wild Zone Alpha pool. Ordinary Dratini at these roofs is Lv. 28–29. The Alpha is not guaranteed on every visit.

Start in Vert Sector 6: Look for the ladder on the building beside Restaurant Le Nah, south of Wild Zone 6. Climb it and cross the roof gap to the restaurant. A rolling jump can make the crossing; Roto-Glide is another option.

Try Jaune Sector 5: From Hibernal Pokémon Center, head north to the gardens near Café Kizuna. Climb the garden ladder and use Roto-Glide to cross two roofs south. Players have reported Alpha catches on a roof in this sector.

Other sectors with an Alpha entry: Bleu 9, Jaune 3, Magenta 8 and Rouge 8. These are areas to check, not exact pins. No universal weather rule or per-visit odds are verified.

POSTGAME / MAIN MISSION 40

Three one-time level 70 Alphas

Catch them if you want to keep them. Catching or defeating any of the three Lv. 70 Alphas counts for Mable, but these individual spawns do not return once defeated.

Find Steelix in the centre of Wild Zone 3, Gallade where Venipede normally gather in Wild Zone 5, and Pangoro on the high rooftop where Mawile normally appears in Wild Zone 9.

Already defeated one? Gallade and Pangoro can reappear as rotating Alphas in Wild Zone 20. For Alpha Steelix, trade an Alpha Onix holding a Metal Coat or check 4★ Steel Hyperspace Wild Zones with Mega Dimension.

Follow Mission 40.

INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED / SIDE MISSION 071

Catch the Lv. 50 Alpha Watchog

On Autumnal Avenue in Magenta Sector 2, Chuckie asks you to clear an Alpha Watchog from the Holovator. Catching this red-eyed level 50 Watchog also completes the mission; you do not have to knock it out. If you are collecting Alphas, catch it during the mission rather than defeating it.

Open Side Mission 71 for the route, rewards and photo-map caveat. This is an independently verified mission opportunity, not another fixed Wild Zone spawn.

OFFICIAL POKÉMON HOME GIFTS

Three Alpha starter Pokémon

Move a Pokémon caught in Z-A into Pokémon HOME for the first time using the Nintendo Switch HOME app. On the mobile HOME app, claim the Alpha Chikorita, Tepig and Totodile Mystery Gifts. Link both apps to the same Nintendo Account.

The gifts are separate from Wild Zone catches. Pokémon HOME transfer guide (full standalone recovery pending).

SIDE MISSION 27 / FOSSILS

Repeat fossil restorations

After Side Mission 27, Reg at the Pokémon Research Lab can restore your Old Amber, Jaw Fossil or Sail Fossil again. Subsequent revivals can generate an Alpha 1% of the time; this is a random chance, not a guaranteed reward.

Use the Stone Emporium for fossils; take them to the lab’s restoration service.

Legendary and mythical encounters described in the original fieldbook are not available as Alphas in those routes. An Alpha that can evolve keeps its Alpha status as it evolves. A fixed city Alpha can usually be revisited; the three anomalous Mission 40 encounters cannot be repeated.

MEGA DIMENSION / HYPERSPACE ALPHAS

Match the portal, then roll Alpha Power

Match a distortion’s Pokémon type, star tier and available species before spending your donut. Hyperspace layouts and spawned species can vary. Sweet-leaning donut ingredients can increase your chance of receiving Alpha Power, but the power itself and a particular desired species are not guaranteed.
