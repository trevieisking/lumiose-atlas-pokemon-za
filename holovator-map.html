How the evidence works

The original player photos record the ground entrance, one or more north-up game maps, and the rooftop arrival wherever available. Green Wild Zone areas and blue battle/map icons are not additional lift identities. Repeated photographs of the Looker Bureau lift are grouped under the original location, not counted twice.

Seventeen survey entries already have 90 genuine JPG photographs on GitHub main. The last 11 photos covering Wild Zone 6 and Café Cyclone are packaged for binary import; two older survey entries have written owner evidence but their photo binaries are not yet hosted. Every unavailable image is clearly marked, never presented as a broken gallery.

The fieldbook covers the lifts the owner encountered; it does not claim that all 23 possible game devices have been matched. Precise roof exits and ladders remain separate work. Neither the original ChatGPT Site nor Supabase, feedback, ratings or player progress is changed by this independent GitHub map.

Ladders, scaffolding and roof routes

Ladders and scaffold access points are not individually identified on the owner's in-game city map screenshots, so this guide does not invent precise pins for them. Use a photographed lift to identify the correct building first. For additional roof access, follow only locally verified directions and look for visible climbable structures near the entrance, courtyard or roof edge. An untested climb remains unverified; an apparent nearby roof is not automatically connected.
