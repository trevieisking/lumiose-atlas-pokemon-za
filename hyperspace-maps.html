IDENTIFY YOUR DISTORTION

Four kinds of Hyperspace destination

Choose the plan that matches the portal you actually entered. These are game-mode descriptions, not four promises of particular street layouts.

✳

Hyperspace Wild Zone

Explore for Pokémon and complete the tasks shown in the zone. Tasks can include captures, clearing obstacles, or collecting sparkling items. Wild Zones can be small, medium or large; the specific route and spawn groups vary.

Watch the timer and keep an efficient loop from the portal entrance.

⚔

Hyperspace Battle Zone

Work through Trainer encounters and the displayed survey objectives. Missions may call for victories or particular move types. Some Trainers offer useful gifts, healing or resources after the battle.

Prepare battle coverage before spending the donut’s timer.

✦

Rogue Mega arena

These are difficult timed boss encounters rather than ordinary item-search maps. Use the boss fieldbook and pay attention to attack windows, the arena and your remaining time.

Do not follow a Wild Zone pickup route into a boss arena.

◇

Unknown or special area

Special story and postgame portals can contain unusual encounters or Mega Stone rewards. Follow the mission objective and marker for that specific entry; do not assume a repeatable standard floor plan.

Check the current mission rather than guessing from the portal icon alone.

Recognise a small, medium or large Wild Zone

Wild Zone size changes how much ground you must cover before Hoopa brings you back. These are planning categories, not three exact maps.

Small

Keep a short sweep. Identify the exit and any immediate targets before wandering away from the entry point.

Medium

Prioritise the listed tasks, then circle back for floating Poké Balls or optional pickups while time remains.

Large

Plan a longer loop, note bridges and elevation changes in the in-game map, and allow enough donut duration for the return.

PRACTICAL ROUTE / TIMED EXPEDITIONS

Survey without getting lost

Use the actual in-game map to find your position. These directions work without pretending a missing rooftop or item dot is known.

  1. 01
    Check the portal before entering.

    Note whether it is a Wild Zone, Battle Zone, boss arena or special area. Check its star rating and the Pokémon or activity you want to tackle.

  2. 02
    Prepare at Hotel Z.

    Use Ansha’s donut system and the guide’s berry reference to select suitable powers and enough exploration time for the intended task.

  3. 03
    Locate the entrance and exit route.

    On arrival, open the in-game map. Check the nearest Holovator or accessible stairs if the current layout includes them. Never assume a ladder or rooftop path exists because one appeared on a different map.

  4. 04
    Follow the displayed survey objectives first.

    Wild Zone tasks may ask for captures, sparkling pickups or obstacle removal; battle tasks may request victories or specific move types. Tasks and item placements can vary, so follow the current in-game objectives.

  5. 05
    Collect, return and report.

    Use remaining time for useful floating Poké Balls and pickups. Return to the city when the timer expires or the objective is done, then report any story survey milestones to Corbeau.

Things worth checking on a Hyperspace survey

Wild Pokémon

Catch the target species or types shown in the current mission and watch for Alpha encounters.

Floating Poké Balls

Break or collect the marked pickups where available. They can provide resources, including Hyper Berries.

Obstacles and sparkles

Look for breakable obstacles and sparkling items when your task list asks for them.

Trainer objectives

In battle zones, look for a particular type of move, a successful knockout or another displayed objective.

The current portal’s on-screen task list takes priority over any general advice here.
