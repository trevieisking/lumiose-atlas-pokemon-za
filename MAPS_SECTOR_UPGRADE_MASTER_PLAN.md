## What the owner has asked for

Upgrade the **existing** Maps / Sector & Wild Zone guide, not a wholesale redesign or an invented page recovery. Use the **ten owner-supplied 21 November 2026 photographs of the in-game Lumiose map** and the existing game-map asset to map **every named city sector**, not just the numbered Wild Zones. Players must be able to zoom in/out and inspect visible building footprints, alleys/gaps, streets and landmarks. Then use that geography as bearings: **Holovators first, written ladder/scaffold/rooftop directions second, verified fixed item and Colorful Screw positions last**. Supply concise walking/rooftop directions and source/verification status for each destination. Keep city-sector and Wild Zone mapping as separate selectable views on one consistent Lumiose base geography.

The photographs are **ten overlapping views of ONE in-game city map**, not ten different city maps and not automatically one image per sector. A **sector-specific, readable zoom/crop of the aligned city source** can fulfil the navigation requirement; do **not** fabricate “48 original screenshots” or require 48 distinct file assets where the geography can be represented faithfully using the same city image. The original screenshots remain **conversation attachments**; they are documented evidence, **not committed image assets** in this repository. Any future image import must have provenance and an approved, tested asset path.

## Binding owner scope clarification — AT-294 (24 November 2026)

**Ladders, scaffolds, bridges and rooftop crossings are guide directions, not an exhaustive map-marker layer.** The owner has clarified that plotting every ladder or scaffold is unnecessary and would clutter the map. Use stable street/sector landmarks, Holovators, written step-by-step directions and photographs where useful to explain how to reach a destination. No new individual ladder, scaffold or bridge pins are authorised by this plan; do not require their exact start/end XY, floor coordinates, citywide count, or separate photo for every structure. Preserve the already-working Hotel Z ladder pilot and original map keys/records as legacy evidence; do not expand that pilot into an inventory. Holovator markers and supported fixed collectible/item pins remain separate mapping priorities with their existing evidence gates. Day-changing Hyperspace distortions remain unpinned. This controlling rule supersedes any older checkpoint, phase wording or optional renderer capability that implies exhaustive physical ascent mapping or future individual ladder/scaffold pins.
