Hotel Z · Right of the front doors

Stand outside Hotel Z and face its entrance. Go into the passage on your right, towards the narrow walled courtyard. The tall pale metal ladder stands vertically against an ivy-covered wall, with bushes at its base.

Map position: approximately X 1202, Y 1556.

The red ring in the owner's two uploaded map screenshots centres on this location, and the close-up image shows the same spot. The avatar was standing at the ladder; map-symbol centre may be slightly offset from the actual foot.
