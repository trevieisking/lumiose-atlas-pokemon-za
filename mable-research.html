Mable’s research & rewards

Start collecting Research Points as soon as Mable gives you the Pokédex. Follow practical tasks throughout Lumiose, collect all 49 level prizes and work towards the Shiny Charm.

Start after you get the Pokédex

Mable runs the Pokémon Research Lab. Her research requests cover Pokédex entries, different type catches, Wild Zone surveys, evolutions, Trainer battles, Mega Crystal breaks and other activities. Completing a request adds Research Points towards your next Research Level.

Where to claim: Press X → Mable’s Research. Check which requests you have advanced, then select any available level rewards. Level 1 is your starting level; the listed prizes begin at Level 2.

Once you unlock the Rewards feature of Mable’s Research, X → Link Play → Mystery Gift also becomes available. Receiving gifts requires an internet connection and a linked Nintendo Account, but not a paid Nintendo Switch Online subscription.

First useful stop: during Main Mission 3, register five species. For the later Kanto starter choice, see Side Mission 22—that request is separate from Mable’s level rewards.

A research routine that fits your playthrough

You do not need to finish every tier of every task. Mix categories as new Wild Zones and story activities become available.

01 / EVERY NEW ZONE

Survey before moving on

Register the available species, then revisit for species that appear at a different time of day. Completing a Wild Zone’s Pokédex roster contributes to its survey research request. Check Wild Zones.

02 / EACH CAPTURE

Catch different types

A new Pokédex registration can advance alongside a type-catch request. When travelling through an area, prioritise uncaught species rather than catching only repeats.

03 / BETWEEN MISSIONS

Evolve extra Pokémon

Level, item and qualifying trade evolutions advance their own categories. Keep the copies needed for the Pokédex, then evolve extras for research progression.

04 / WHILE EXPLORING

Stack small side tasks

Trainer wins, breaking Mega Crystals, visiting cafés, bench rests and fossil restoration can contribute to research. Check your actual target counters in the X menu before making a detour.

05 / BEFORE A TOUGH FIGHT

Collect new TMs

Check your research rewards after each Wild Zone. A new TM may give your team the attacking type it needs for the next Royale promotion or Rogue Mega boss.

06 / THE LATE-GAME GOAL

Finish for the Charm

Work across the tasks you can access instead of forcing story-locked activities early. Level 49 gives the Master Ball and Level 50 gives the Shiny Charm. See more shiny tips.

Why the Shiny Charm matters

The level-50 Shiny Charm improves the odds of eligible wild shiny spawns from roughly 1 in 4,096 to about 1 in 1,024 per spawn. Those are independent chances, not a guaranteed shiny after 1,024 checks. Use a short spawn-reset route and check both colour and sparkle cues.

When the dedicated shiny-hunting fieldbook is recovered, it will cover bench resets and Mega Dimension’s separate Sparkling Power. Until then, the existing secrets page remains available.

If the prize is not showing: check your current Research Level and unclaimed rewards in the X menu. Story-gated, trade-related and optional task categories have different requirements; follow the in-game wording rather than treating every request as mandatory.
