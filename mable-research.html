POKÉMON LEGENDS: Z-A · LAB RESEARCH

Mable's Research
and all level rewards

Earn research points by exploring Lumiose, completing field tasks and registering Pokémon. Find exactly what you earn at each research level without leaving this guide.

← Back to walkthroughGuide overviewSee every reward ↓

How to unlock and claim rewards

  1. Complete the early Pokédex tutorial in Main Mission 3. Catch and register at least five different species, then visit the Pokémon Research Lab as the story instructs.
  2. Open your main menu with X and choose Mable's Research. This is where the available requests, progress and earned rewards appear.
  3. Complete research requests to gain points. Registration, evolution, catching Pokémon of different types, surveying Wild Zones and Mega Evolution-related progress are examples of research activities. Individual tasks have their own requirements, which should be checked in the game menu.
  4. Claim rewards through the Research interface. Increasing the level unlocks the next reward; revisit the menu regularly so you do not overlook TMs and useful items.

You do not need to finish the entire Pokédex before obtaining the Shiny Charm: the unlock is tied to Mable's Research level 50.

What to prioritise while playing

  • Early game: Register new species as you explore, then check Research after each new Wild Zone or batch of evolutions.
  • Mid-game: Use the TM rewards to improve coverage for the next promotion or Rogue Mega battle.
  • Late game: Level 48 gives three Gold Bottle Caps, level 49 gives a Master Ball, and level 50 gives the Shiny Charm.

Research tasks can change as the story advances. This table lists the level rewards, not every tier of every individual request; the request-by-request task catalogue is a separate audit item.
