What still needs verification

These are genuine remaining evidence or source gaps, not a sign that the restored maps have disappeared.

  • Exact sector boundaries, items and rooftop approachesAll 48 sector names are searchable on one city map, but exact sector-centre coordinates, many item and Screw placements, floor levels, ladders and scaffolding ascents still require photographic or gameplay validation.
  • Missing original Hyperspace close-up imagesThe saved Maps PDF referred to nine detailed Hyperspace layouts, but their original image files were not captured. The existing Hyperspace fieldbook gives useful routes without pretending those exact pictures have been recovered.
  • Moving DLC distortions are not permanent map pinsRandom Hyperspace distortions change as the game progresses. The Atlas deliberately does not mark them as fixed city locations; follow your game's active map and mission indicators.
  • The original editable Workspace project is separateThis GitHub edition was reconstructed from a saved browser capture and 19 PDFs (213 printed pages), not exported from the original Workspace application's code. Five exact standalone source pages and the original private runtime were not recovered.
  • Progress across different website addressesSaved guide checkmarks belong to their own site and browser storage. The GitHub edition and original ChatGPT Workspace Site are not automatically synchronised; this page does not reset, move or alter anyone's saved progress.

We improve verified omissions or mistakes. Existing map imagery, player photographs, checklists and live site connections remain protected.

Where this guide's content came from

The independent site preserves 17 recovered main-guide sections, an inventory of 578 fingerprinted saved resources, and source-backed material from 19 saved guide PDFs. The complete technical history remains in the GitHub repository as RECOVERY_STATUS.md; that archive is not intended as a phone-facing guide page.

These figures describe guide coverage, not a guarantee that every in-game location, mission step or DLC feature is fully confirmed. The original Workspace Site has not been overwritten or automatically updated.
