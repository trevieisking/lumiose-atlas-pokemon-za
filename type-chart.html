Two types multiply together

To compare a move with a dual-type Pokémon, multiply its two defending matchups. Water attacking Fire/Ground is 2 × 2 = 4×; Electric attacking Water/Ground is 2 × 0 = 0×. Two resistances produce ¼×.

Useful combinations for your adventure

Rock → Fire/Flying = 4×

Useful against a Pokémon with both Fire and Flying typing. Check a Mega form’s actual type first.

Grass → Rock/Water = 4×

A strong option when preparing for Rogue Mega Barbaracle.

Ice → Dragon/Flying = 4×

Helpful for Rogue Mega Dragonite if you can find a safe opening.

Fighting → Normal/Ghost = 0×

An ordinary type immunity overrides even a super-effective half of a dual typing; special encounter rules can differ.
