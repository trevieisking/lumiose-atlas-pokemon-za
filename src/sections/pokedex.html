Where to get Flabébé

City capture route: For Side Mission 12, collect one ordinary Flabébé of each flower colour. Red: search Wild Zone 3, especially the central and eastern garden. Yellow: check the grass along Vert Street between Prism Tower and Vert Plaza, or the flowers by the bridge south of Wild Zone 6. White: enter the courtyard in Vert Sector 3 and check the statues and fountain; make sure it is Flabébé, not Floette. Orange: search the roadside flower beds on Bleu Street near the entrance to Passage de la Félicité, with roofs near Wild Zone 6 as another reported route. Blue: from the Racine Construction area in Vert Sector 6, head northwest to the ladder and search the roof; Magenta Sector 4 is another reported Holovator approach. Published ordinary bands include blue Lv. 9–12 in Vert 6, blue Lv. 10–12 in Bleu 7, white Lv. 10–13 in Bleu 4 and orange Lv. 10–13 in Bleu 8. Take each colour to Vivace for the hair-colour unlocks. For Alphas, the separate Jaune rooftop guide covers all five colours; these are not the ordinary colour spawns.

Conditions still unverified: These are documented colour routes and additional sector-level records, not an exhaustive list of every colour slot. Individual day/night, weather, precise access unlocks and respawn probabilities remain unverified.
