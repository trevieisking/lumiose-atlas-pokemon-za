20WILD ZONES

Open the Alpha Pokédex · 364 species

01Unlock

Complete Side Mission 1, “A Big Ol’ Bunnelby,” during Main Mission 5. Catch or defeat the Lv. 10 Alpha in the alley east of Wild Zone 4. First Alpha route. Finish a zone survey for additional random Alpha chances there.

02Register the trip

Physically visit a Pokémon Center before trying to fast travel back to it. Check that it has registered on your map; taxis can reach places you have not visited.

03Catch

Check the target’s time of day, approach from cover, or battle to weaken it. Catch the one-time Mission 40 Alphas instead of defeating them.

BEYOND THE WILD ZONES

Every kind of Alpha route.

Fixed street encounters, one-time story Alphas, fossil restorations, HOME gifts, and the changing Mega Dimension portal pools all belong in your Alpha hunt.

City districts

Bleu District: Pansage, Pansear, Panpour · Jaune District: Garbodor, Flabébé, Pidgeot · Magenta District: Swirlix, Kakuna · Rouge District: Pansage, Pansear, Panpour, Spritzee · Vert District: Patrat, Trubbish · Lysandre Labs: Arbok

Find Meadow and Garden Alpha Vivillon

Meadow Pattern, base game: After the respective zone survey, look for Alpha Scatterbug (Lv. 15–16) in Wild Zone 1, Alpha Spewpa (Lv. 22–24) by day in Wild Zone 4, or Alpha Vivillon (Lv. 46–47) by day in Wild Zone 13. Wild Zone 20 can also roll Meadow Vivillon (Lv. 63–64) in its central pool.

Garden Pattern, Mega Dimension: In 1★ Flying portals, look for Alpha Scatterbug (Lv. 19–21), Spewpa (Lv. 24–26) or Vivillon (Lv. 24–26). In 1★ Bug portals, look for Alpha Scatterbug (Lv. 21–23), Spewpa (Lv. 22–24) or Vivillon (Lv. 24–26). These are catch levels. Check the portal roster; a particular catch is not guaranteed.

Level up Scatterbug at Lv. 9 or above and Spewpa at Lv. 12 or above to keep the pattern and Alpha status. The one-off museum Spewpa instead evolves into Marine Pattern. See Rewards for Poké Ball Pattern Vivillon. The earlier 16-pattern Spewpa distribution has ended; neither gift is presented as a wild Alpha route.

See Vivillon’s Pokédex entry.

Find all five Alpha Flabébé colours

Red or white, Jaune Sector 2: From the Wild Zone 8 gate, follow Jaune Street to the alley behind Justice Dojo. Clear the rocks with a Fighting-type move, then climb the ladder to the roof for a Lv. 20–23 Alpha.

Yellow, orange or blue, Jaune Sector 3: Climb the rooftop obstacle course to the building roof for a Lv. 21–23 Alpha. Each active rooftop spot gives one Alpha, not every colour at once. Clear daytime is a documented time to try, not a proven requirement.

Same-colour evolutions: Level up Flabébé to Lv. 19 or higher to get Floette, then use a Shiny Stone for Florges. Both colour and Alpha status carry over. Eternal Flower Floette is a separate story gift and cannot evolve.

  • Base-game alternatives after the respective survey: Wild Zone 7 has red Alpha Floette (Lv. 31–32) and orange (Lv. 31–33). In Wild Zone 16, check the fountain area by day for blue or white Alpha Florges (Lv. 52–53).
  • DLC Fairy portals, all five colours: At 2★, Alpha Flabébé is Lv. 25–27. At 3★, Flabébé is Lv. 29–31, Floette Lv. 33–35 and Florges Lv. 37–39. At 4★, Floette is Lv. 46–48 and Florges Lv. 48–50. These are Alpha catch levels. Check the portal’s roster; a particular colour or Alpha is not guaranteed.

Only collecting colours for Vivace? Follow the early ordinary-colour route for Side Mission 012.

Mission 40: catch, do not defeat

Steelix in Zone 3 · Gallade in Zone 5 · Pangoro in Zone 9. Each level 70 encounter is one-time. Catch it before completing the objective.

Side Mission 43: In Magenta Sector 3, catch the garden’s daytime Alpha Weedle. Decline Grandt’s request if you want to keep it; either choice completes the mission. See the mission catch details.

Side Mission 180 (DLC): Rosebud is a Lv. 99 Alpha Annihilape in Wild Zone 6, but cannot be caught. For your own Alpha Annihilape, catch an Alpha Primeape in a suitable 3★ Fighting portal and use Rage Fist 20 times before choosing Evolve. See the full route.

HOME and fossils

Deposit a Z-A-caught Pokémon into HOME and claim the three Alpha starter gifts in the mobile app.

Compare fossil Alpha routes

At the lab: After Side Mission 27, buy fossils at the Stone Emporium: Jaw Fossil for Tyrunt (₽20,000), Sail Fossil for Amaura (₽20,000), or Old Amber for Aerodactyl (₽30,000). Take them to Reg on the second floor of the Pokémon Research Lab. Each repeat revival has a 1% Alpha chance. Ordinary revivals are Lv. 20. If you see Lv. 35, check for the Alpha mark.

With Mega Dimension: Tyrunt and Amaura can appear as Alphas in different 3★ Rock portals, before Special Scans are available. To evolve them, level up Tyrunt at Lv. 39 or higher by day, or Amaura at Lv. 39 or higher by night.

  • 3★ Rock: Tyrunt and Amaura, Lv. 24–26 ordinary or Lv. 33–35 Alpha.
  • 4★ Ice: Amaura, Lv. 37–39 ordinary or Lv. 46–48 Alpha.
  • 5★ Rock: Aerodactyl, Lv. 53–55 ordinary or Lv. 62–64 Alpha; Tyrunt and Amaura, Lv. 52–54 or Lv. 61–63 Alpha; Tyrantrum and Aurorus, Lv. 55–57 or Lv. 64–66 Alpha.

Those are catch levels, not boosted battle levels. For 5★ Rock, unlock Special Scans. Each scan costs 25,000 Hyperspace Survey Points and the result is random; it may not give a Rock portal.

MEGA DIMENSION / HYPERSPACE ALPHAS

Match the portal, then roll Alpha Power.

Open a portal with the species’ type and star tier shown in our Hyperspace Pokédex. The layout and Alpha spawns change. Sweet donuts can roll Alpha Power: 1% base, 11% at Lv. 1, 21% at Lv. 2, and 51% at Lv. 3 per eligible spawn. The power and target species are not guaranteed.

Legendary and mythical encounters listed in the full fieldbook cannot be Alpha. Evolve an Alpha pre-evolution for Alpha evolved forms. Fixed city Alphas can be revisited; anomalous Mission 40 Alphas cannot.
