07 / PLAY SMARTER

The essentials,
in one place.

Six habits that make Lumiose smoother from the first night to the postgame.

01

Day / night rhythm

Daylight is for Wild Zone catches, healing, items and team building. At night, enter the red battle zone, earn points and claim a Challenger’s Ticket for a promotion match.

02

Real-time battles

Moves launch on command, with timing, range and area to consider. Keep the trainer moving, watch attack tells and switch Pokémon when a matchup turns bad.

03

Catch more safely

Approach from behind or out of sight. A thrown ball can catch directly; if that fails, battle, lower HP and try again. Some species flee when they spot you.

04

Mega Evolution

A Pokémon needs its matching Mega Stone. Build Mega Power in battle, then use the transformation for a strong opening against tougher trainers and Rogue Megas.

05

Alpha hunting

Guaranteed Alphas have fixed species and zone placements. Other species can roll as Alphas after the relevant Wild Zone survey is finished. Bring a durable team and better balls.

06

After the credits

Missions 38–42 continue the story through Infinite Royale and legendary encounters. Mega Dimension is a separate paid expansion with Hyperspace, Hoopa and donut making.

Evolution, friendship and Link Trade

Normal evolution: press X to open the party, select a Pokémon that meets its evolution condition and choose Evolve. Level thresholds mean at least that level; you do not need to gain another level merely to trigger an already satisfied requirement. For stone evolutions, select the correct Evolution Stone from the Bag and apply it to the eligible Pokémon. Do not confuse Evolution Stones with held Mega Stones.

Where to buy stones: the Stone Emporium on Vernal Avenue sells standard Evolution Stones for ₽3,000 each as stock becomes available. Some stones are progression-gated; check the shop and look for item pickups or quest rewards if a stone is not yet on sale. Stone Emporium

Friendship: the evolution threshold is 160. Bring the target out as your active Pokémon and buy a café drink to increase its friendship, or sit on a bench for at least ten seconds to help your whole party. Recheck the party menu after meeting any time or move requirement. After Side Mission 25, the woman with Roselia at Café Pokémon-Amie in Magenta Sector 4 checks friendship. Side Mission 36 at Café Rouleau in Magenta Sector 5 rewards the Soothe Bell; equip it on your target. No requirement to reach the maximum 255 friendship for these evolutions.

Link Trade: once unlocked, press X, open Link Play and choose Link Trade. Choose Nearby Players for local wireless or Faraway Players for internet. Both players enter the same eight-digit code, offer their Pokémon and inspect the other offer before confirming. Internet trading requires Nintendo Switch Online; local trade does not. Use matching game updates. If you want your original Pokémon back, arrange a return trade with a trusted partner before sending it. When a held item is required, equip it before the trade. A trade evolution can occur on receipt; check the party menu for Evolve if needed. No need to assume strangers will return your Pokémon. Nintendo multiplayer instructions

Trade items: Side Mission 10 awards a Sachet, 14 a Whipped Dream, 84 a King’s Rock and 101 a Metal Coat. Use the quest buttons on individual Pokédex entries for exact routes. Some trade evolutions can also be caught fully evolved, including rotating Wild Zone 20 Alphas. A rotating candidate is not a guaranteed catch. Check the relevant species' capture records instead of assuming every trade species has a wild alternative.

Special conditions: use a bench to switch day/night. For Goodra, wait for rain, not just a time change. For Pangoro, include a Dark-type teammate. Inkay must be evolved in handheld mode with the console inverted. Check the desired evolution carefully before confirming, particularly Eevee, flower colours and regional forms.
