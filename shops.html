Sell ordinary valuables to raise money for medicines, training, or your next outfit. At a Pokémon Center shop, choose the selling option and inspect the Treasures pocket. Review the item and quantity before confirming; do not sell everything indiscriminately.

Base game

Collect sparkling ground items on your normal exploration route, complete missions, and fight in the nighttime Z-A Royale. Big Nuggets are among possible Infinite Royale reward-match prizes. Upgrade the Gold Canari Plush at Racine Construction to improve prize money.

Mega Dimension

Use a sour-flavour donut and check that it has Item Power for treasures before entering Hyperspace. Big Haul Power is a useful extra if it appears. Break floating Poké Balls with Pokémon attacks and complete survey tasks for the golden Bonus Ball. Four- and five-star zones can yield Big Nuggets with the appropriate Item Power. Rewards vary: neither a specific donut power nor a fixed profit is guaranteed. Choose a zone your team can handle and leave safely before the timer runs out. See our berry and donut guide and Mega Dimension guide.

What to keep

Check each item's purpose. Old Amber and other fossils, evolution items, Bottle Caps, healing items, Hyper Berries and other useful resources should not be treated as spare cash unless you are sure you no longer need them. Mega Shards and Colorful Screws have their own exchange systems, not a Pokédollar selling route.

MEGA CRYSTALS / CURRENCY ROUTE

Mega Shards explained

EARN THEM

Break pink Mega Crystals

Find crystal clusters around Lumiose and order a Pokémon to attack them. Clusters return over time, so a rooftop or city-sector loop is more efficient than waiting at one spot. Mega Shards are separate from Pokédollars and Colorful Screws.

Early investment: the Pink Canari Plush increases the number of Shards gained per crystal. Upgrading it to Lv. 3 makes larger purchases less grindy.

SPEND THEM

Visit Quasartico Inc.

After Main Mission 10, speak to the Mega Shard Exchange receptionist at Quasartico Inc. Stock includes training items, candies and Mega Stones, but more stones become available as the story progresses.

In the Mega Dimension DLC, a different trader in the Vert Sector 7 alley by Wild Zone 17 sells TMs, Feathers and eventually the four Genesect Drives.

Separate goals: all four Genesect Drives cost 4 × 540 = 2,160 Mega Shards. The later Magearna request needs another 999 Shards given to its quest NPC. The calculator treats these as different goals; neither is an automatic free reward.

SOUTH BOULEVARD / RACINE CONSTRUCTION

Spend Colorful Screws wisely

Each Canari Plush offers a passive boost. Upgrade each colour in sequence: 3 screws for Lv. 1, 5 for Lv. 2, then 8 for Lv. 3 (16 total for one colour if starting from zero). After reaching Royale Rank Y, the first Red Lv. 1 plush is awarded for free. That means 77 screws are needed for the remaining upgrades from that starting point—not 80.

Pink

More Mega Shards from crystals. Useful before buying costly stones, Genesect Drives or saving for Magearna.

Blue

Improved capture success rate. Good for rare or fleeing Pokémon and Alphas.

Red

More battle experience. Useful when raising teams for missions and Royale promotions.

Gold

More battle prize money and medal conversion. Helps finance expensive shop items.

Green

Increases your Trainer’s survivability by raising max HP.

Set your current plush levels and the screws you hold. It will calculate the cost to max all five. The defaults assume you've already received Red Lv. 1 from the story. This planner makes no changes to save data.

All-100 collectible goal: Lumiose contains 100 Colorful Screws. Find all 100 and complete the plush exchanges, then speak to the Racine worker for the giant decorative Canari Plush for Hotel Z. Buying plushes does not replace the requirement to find all 100 unique screws.

Recommended shopping order

BEFORE A WILD ZONE

Prioritise Balls and healing

Buy a suitable stock of standard Balls at a Pokémon Center. Add a few Quick, Dusk, Net or Repeat Balls from Autumnal Avenue when your target warrants them; healing items are easier to replenish before a long route.

BEFORE A ROGUE MEGA

Buy for a weakness

Use the complete Type Chart and Rogue Mega Boss guide to choose a move and counter first. Buy a Mega Stone only if the correct species, unlock and currency are available.

BEFORE A LONG GRIND

Boost your resource gains

Invest collected Colorful Screws in the Pink plush to improve Mega Shard farming, then consider Gold for Pokédollars or Blue for harder Alpha captures. All effects are passive when obtained.

BEFORE DLC SIDE QUESTS

Save for the real totals

Plan separately for the four Genesect Drives (2,160 Shards) and Magearna’s 999-Shard requirement. Prioritise a useful quest before spending your currency on optional stones or TMs.
